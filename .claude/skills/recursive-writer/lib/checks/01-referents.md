# 01 Referents

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** each pronoun (it, its, them, this, that, both), first-mention "the X", count or grouping
(name its items when a nearby list differs), time word (already, later, still, afterwards), sequence
word (first, then, before: its other step in the same sentence or the next), relative word (old,
new, original, previous: its anchor in the same sentence, still true after repeated changes), and
possessive (your, their own) points to exactly one thing.
**Evidence:** word = its antecedent, quoted from the text before it.

- The nearest preceding noun that fits is the referent. If it's the wrong one, fail.
- A heading, callout, list item, screenshot, code block, or two medium-length sentences break the
  chain: repeat the noun, or move the sentence next to it. After any move, re-check the referents.
- Named controls, settings, and calls: use the name, even when a pronoun would resolve. Two actions on
  two objects name each object.
- Specific things the reader provides take "the" ("the images you attach"). A generic instance takes
  "a", and later mentions take "its". Something the reader may have several of takes the plural at
  first mention.
- Don't flag: a pronoun in the sentence right after its noun, when that noun is the only match.
