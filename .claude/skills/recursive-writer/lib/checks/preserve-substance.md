# Preserve substance

**Applies when:** a change to existing or approved text (never the skill's own unseen draft).
**Flags:** --change
**Requires:** no rewrite, restructure, simplification, or cut loses anything the reader needs:
conditions, consequences, the explanation itself, and every link survive. A fact the reader can't act
on stays only if they'll see its effect; judge by the least-informed reader likely to land here.
**Evidence:** the facts in the original, listed; for each, where it lands in the new version, or why
it was cut. For a fact the reader can't act on, the effect they see (in the UI, in their data, in
what their users get), or "none".

- Effect seen: keep it only when the reader would misread what they see without it, easy to read;
  state the effect, not the internals behind it. No effect
  (internal numbers, codes, check order, retry intervals, internal names): cut it, don't move it.
- Reference section: each fact comes with what the reader does with it; a bare list of facts becomes
  advice, or goes.
- A fact with no home (after a restructure or a move) gets one: the nearest fitting section, or a new
  section.
- A core takeaway gets visible weight.
- The word that makes a sentence true survives: quote it from the original, and find it in the
  rewrite.
