# Misconception guard

**Applies when:** an article or section opener; a feature description.
**Triggers:** ^#{2,6}\s
**Flags:** --opener
**Requires:** openers and feature descriptions head off the likeliest wrong assumption.
**Evidence:** the most likely wrong assumption a reader brings to this feature, or "none".

- Address it early, where it would mislead, inside an existing sentence; no disclaimer sentence that
  the previous sentence already implies.
