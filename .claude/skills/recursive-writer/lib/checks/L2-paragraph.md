# L2 Paragraph

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** the paragraph runs in a linear order, earns its place, and fits its neighbors.

**Linearity.** Evidence: the link between each pair of adjacent sentences (because, so, then, for
example, except). No link: the sentence is misplaced, or a step is missing.
- Cause before effect, rule before exception, common case before edge case, principle before example,
  use case before constraint, the current state before the change.
- Cause first yields when the cause is a technicality the reader never sees (lead with the visible
  effect, or drop the cause).
- Lead with the point: what the reader does or most needs, then the reason, unless the reason is the
  condition. A section opens with its value. Within a sentence too: the deciding factor is the subject
  ("Y determines X", not "X depends on Y"). Outside a step, the subject comes first: "In the X," opens
  only a step.
- Introduce a term, an entity, or a UI element before using it; no forward references. Evidence: the
  line where the article first introduces it. If that line comes later, fail, even for a UI label the
  reader could recognize.
- Prerequisite before action: an instruction comes after what the reader needs to carry it out.
  Warnings and conditions come before the action they govern; the scope or qualifier comes first.
- Say what happens before (or instead of) what doesn't, except when a limitation, or a difference from
  what the reader already knows, is the reason the unit exists: then it leads ("You can't type a custom
  tag into a text element.").
- Adjacent paragraphs, or an instruction and the advice after it, never seem to contradict each other.

**Value.** Evidence: the reader who needs the paragraph, and what they can do after reading it. Not
the typical reader: the first words say who it's for. No reader: cut (see preserve-substance).

**Fit.** Same tone, depth, and terms as the neighbors; the register depends on where the sentence sits
(intros plain, mechanism definitions technical). Repetition between sentences: see 08.
