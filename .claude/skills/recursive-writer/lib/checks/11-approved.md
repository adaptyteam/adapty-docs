# 11 Approved wording

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** reuse what's approved; never bring back what was rejected.
**Evidence:** the approved wording found for this idea and its source (`main`, a unit, section, or
article mark in `ledger.md`, a hand edit in `hand-edits.md`), or "none".

- Approved text is (1) what shipped on `main`, (2) the units, sections, and articles marked done in
  the ticket's `ledger.md`, and (3) the writer's hand edits in `hand-edits.md`. Never reword it
  unless the writer asks or a fact in it is wrong. Other text, the skill's own applied proposals
  included, isn't approved: run it through the checks before you reuse it.
- "Apply" doesn't approve the final wording: the writer often edits the text after an apply. When the
  writer moves on to the next item, each unit the item changed gets a mark with the unit's text at
  that moment, the writer's hand edits included. A section gets its mark when the writer says it's
  done. The article gets its mark when the work moves on to the next file, or when the writer says
  the file is done.
- The marks refresh at file done: `ledger.py file-done` brings every mark in the file to the live
  text.
- Approved wording is reused verbatim wherever the same idea recurs.
- Model new content on the writer's manual edits in `hand-edits.md`; after the writer edits the
  source, update every copy.
- Rejected stays rejected: the strike list holds it; never re-propose a rejected version or its
  structure with swapped words.
- In a `hand-edits.md` entry, the writer's version is approved and the skill's version is rejected. A
  fact the writer removed by hand counts as rejected: never re-raise it, in text or in "Also noticed".
