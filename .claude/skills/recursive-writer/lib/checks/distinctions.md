# Distinctions

**Applies when:** a definition, description, comparison, benefit, or list of options or cases.
**Triggers:** \b(instead of|unlike|than|differ\w*|compared|versus|vs\.?|both|either)\b
**Requires:** definitions, descriptions, comparisons, benefits, and lists of options or cases tell
their subject apart from its neighbors.
**Evidence:** the nearest look-alike, and the words that exclude it. If the same words would also
describe the look-alike, rewrite.

- Frame cases by the difference that matters to the reader, not the system's internal criterion.
- Like with like: contrast pairs and list items are the same kind and scale.
- Comparisons name both sides fully. For two cases that behave differently, say what each one does to
  the result. A benefit or drawback that steers the reader follows from its stated cause, names the
  actual loss, and survives "what if I never use this?": if nothing breaks, say what is deferred
  instead.
