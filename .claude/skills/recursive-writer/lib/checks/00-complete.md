# 00 Complete sentence

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** every clause has its own verb and nouns; the sentence makes sense alone.
**Evidence:** lift the sentence out of its paragraph and restate its claim. If the restatement needs
words from the neighbors, it fails.

- Watch: clauses that borrow a verb or noun from another sentence ("which ones", "the same", "do so",
  "as well", "the rest").
- A comparative names what it's compared to.
- Table cells complete their column header grammatically.
- No circular causes; no sentence that consumes its own subject.
- Pronouns that point back are 01's job.
