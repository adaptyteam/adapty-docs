# 12 Simplest version

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** the shortest, simplest text that keeps every fact the reader needs.
**Evidence:** what you tried removing, and why each remaining part stays; for a fix, the word count
before and after, and the fact that justifies any added words.

- Before adding anything, try removing: a sentence, a clause, a step, a qualifier, an enumeration, an
  example the reader doesn't need to recognize or apply the rule. A sentence that only explains the
  step before it folds into that step. Length is a symptom: cut what no reader needs, true facts included (a cut fact
  stays in the session's `facts.md`).
- A simple concept or action gets a short text; don't over-explain it or make it sound complicated.
