# Frame check

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** once per article, a frame; for every sentence, a one-line check that it still belongs.
**Evidence:** the frame (started before the first stage, completed at gate 2): the article's promise
(title, description), its reader, and the reader's path: the article's path line, then one line per
section on what the reader does there, starting from where the previous section leaves them (for a
branch, from its condition: "If …"). Per sentence: the section's path line and the article promise it
serves, or the user request it answers. A sentence that takes the reader from the previous section
into this one serves its section's path.
None: cut the sentence; its fact stays in `facts.md`.

- Before you draft a new article or section, the frame needs its path lines. If they're missing,
  write them from the approved `outline.md` first.
- If a sentence may not belong in its section or article, load `L3` and `L4` and run them.
