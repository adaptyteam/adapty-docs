# 02 One reading

**Applies when:** always.
**Requires:** each key word means one specific thing, to a non-developer and to a developer with a
server background.
**Evidence:** each word with another plausible reading: that reading, then keep or rewrite.

- Vague or double-meaning word (own, give, take, replace, some): replace it with the literal, specific
  word, or say what happens in practice and who does it. For "some", name the items the reader
  handles.
- The predicate fits the subject (a day can't exceed a limit); the object is the specific type when
  the action accepts only that type; prepositions match the real
  relationship and the UI interaction (from a dropdown); complete clause first (no garden paths; two
  noun/verb homographs side by side always fail: "app labels value").
- Near concepts stay apart: no value vs an empty value.
- A phrase that reads as an instrument ("Open a placement with the flow") gets an explicit relation ("a
  placement that serves the flow"). Relation verbs between entities match how the product connects
  them, and name both ends, in the order the reader meets them.
- Labels and headings: no noun with a strong meaning outside the docs. Reuse the original label, or
  name the action.
- Skip: established technical and Adapty terms in their documented sense (event, property,
  placement).
