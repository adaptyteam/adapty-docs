When a player taps a button with a [custom action](onboarding-actions#custom-actions), your app code receives the ID of this action, with no other data. Your app code then shows a rewarded ad over the flow.

To offer diamonds for an ad, add a button with the text "Watch an ad for 5 diamonds". In the **Interactions** tab of the button, click **Add trigger** > **On tap**, then click **Add action** > **Custom Action**. Enter the action ID `watch_ad`. Keep the number on the button equal to the ad reward that your backend adds.
