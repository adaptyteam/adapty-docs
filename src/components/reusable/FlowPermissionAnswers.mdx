After your app passes the result, the flow runs the **After response** actions. The default action is **Navigate Next**, so users go to the next screen whether they grant the permission or decline it. Without an **After response** action, users stay on the screen.

To send users who grant the permission and users who decline it to different screens, add actions to **When allowed** and **When denied**. The flow then runs these actions instead of the **After response** actions:

- **When allowed** actions: After the user grants the permission.
- **When denied** actions: After your app passes `denied`, `restricted`, or `permanently_denied` to the flow.

**Do nothing** alone counts as no actions. For example, with only **Do nothing** in **When denied**, users who decline still go to the next screen.
