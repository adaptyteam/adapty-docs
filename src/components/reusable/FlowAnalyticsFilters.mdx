The flow metrics page covers views, completions, conversion, and revenue. Metrics such as MRR, active subscriptions, churn, grace period, and billing issues exist only on the [Charts](charts) page. To see them for a particular flow, filter these metrics by **Flow** or **Flow screen**:

- **Flow**: One flow, with all its screens counted together.
- **Flow screen**: One screen of a flow. Screens are nested under their flow, and use the following naming convention: `<position>. <screen name>`, for example `9. Paywall A`.

To compare flows or screens side by side on one chart, group by the same attributes instead of filtering.

:::important
The filter lists only flows and screens with at least one transaction.
:::
