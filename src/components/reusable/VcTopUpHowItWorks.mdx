1. A player runs out of energy, and your app code opens the out-of-energy flow. The player sees the diamond balance and two buttons, one per option: an energy refill for 10 diamonds, and an hour of unlimited energy for 30.
2. The player taps a button, and your app code calls your backend to spend the diamonds. While the spend runs, the player sees a spinner in the button.
3. If the spend goes through, the player sees a checkmark. Your app code closes the flow, and the game gives the player the energy.
4. If the balance is too low, your app code closes the out-of-energy flow and opens the top-up flow. The player sees how many more diamonds they need, and buys a diamond pack or the subscription.
5. After the purchase, your app code opens the out-of-energy flow again. The player sees the new balance, and taps a button to spend diamonds.
