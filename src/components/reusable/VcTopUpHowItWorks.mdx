1. A player taps the unlimited energy button, and your app code calls your backend to spend 30 diamonds.
2. If the balance is too low, your backend returns how many more diamonds the player needs.
3. Your app code opens the flow. The player sees how many diamonds are missing, and the current balance.
4. The player buys diamonds or watches an ad. After an ad, the player sees the new balance in the open flow.
5. After a purchase, or after an ad reward that covers the missing diamonds, your app code closes the flow and retries the spend. With enough diamonds, the player receives the energy without a second tap.
