| Permission in the builder | Value | iOS: `Info.plist` key | Android: manifest permission |
|:--------------------------|:------|:----------------------|:-----------------------------|
| Push notifications | `push` | None. To receive push notifications, the Push Notifications capability | `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, Android 13 and later |
| App tracking (ATT) | `tracking` | `NSUserTrackingUsageDescription` | — |
| Location while using the app | `location_when_use` | `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` | `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION`, and `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` for precise location |
| Location always | `location_always` | `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` and `NSLocationAlwaysAndWhenInUseUsageDescription` | `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`, Android 10 and later, with the foreground location permissions |
| Precise location | `location_full_accuracy` | `NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription` and `NSLocationTemporaryUsageDescriptionDictionary` | `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` and `ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` |
| Camera | `camera` | `NSCameraUsageDescription` | `CAMERA` |
| Microphone | `microphone` | `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` | `RECORD_AUDIO` |
| Photos | `photos` | `NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription`, or `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription` if the app only adds photos | `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` and `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, Android 13 and later. `READ_MEDIA_VISUAL_USER_SELECTED`, Android 14 and later. `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, Android 12 and earlier |
| Media library | `media_library` | `NSAppleMusicUsageDescription` | `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`, Android 13 and later. `READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, Android 12 and earlier |
| Apple Music | `music` | `NSAppleMusicUsageDescription` | — |
| Speech recognition | `speech` | `NSSpeechRecognitionUsageDescription`, and `NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` for live audio | `RECORD_AUDIO` |
| Contacts | `contacts` | `NSContactsUsageDescription` | `READ_CONTACTS`, and `WRITE_CONTACTS` to write contacts |
| Calendar | `calendar` | `NSCalendarsFullAccessUsageDescription` or `NSCalendarsWriteOnlyAccessUsageDescription`, and `NSCalendarsUsageDescription` before iOS 17 | `READ_CALENDAR`, and `WRITE_CALENDAR` to add events |
| Reminders | `reminders` | `NSRemindersFullAccessUsageDescription`, and `NSRemindersUsageDescription` before iOS 17 | — |
| Health data | `health` | `NSHealthShareUsageDescription` to read and `NSHealthUpdateUsageDescription` to write, and the HealthKit capability | Health Connect permissions for each data type, such as `android.permission.health.READ_STEPS` |
| Motion & Fitness | `motion` | `NSMotionUsageDescription` | `ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION`, Android 10 and later |
| Bluetooth | `bluetooth` | `NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription` | `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` and `BLUETOOTH_CONNECT`, Android 12 and later, for apps that target Android 12. `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION` otherwise |
| Local network | `local_network` | `NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription`, and `NSBonjourServices` for Bonjour | `ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK`, Android 17 and later, for apps that target Android 17 |
| HomeKit | `homekit` | `NSHomeKitUsageDescription`, and the HomeKit capability | — |
| Focus status | `focus_status` | `NSFocusStatusUsageDescription`, and, to read the status, notification authorization and the Communication Notifications capability | — |
| Siri | `siri` | `NSSiriUsageDescription`, and the Siri capability | — |

A dash means that the OS has no such permission: answer `unsupported`.
