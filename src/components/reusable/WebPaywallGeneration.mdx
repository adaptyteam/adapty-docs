Under the **URL** field, find the **generate one with AI** link. Click it.

Expand the **Generation setup** panel and complete its six steps in order. Each button opens the page where that step happens, so click **Refresh** when you come back.

<ZoomImage id="mail-checkout-prerequisites.webp" width="500px" alt="Generation setup checklist on the Web Paywall create page" />

| Checklist item | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Brand is ready** | Already ticked if you've set up your [brand](mail-brand). If not, click **Set up brand**. |
| **2. Log in to the paywall builder dashboard** | Click **Log in** to authenticate the builder. |
| **3. Stripe account connected** | Click **Connect Stripe** and connect your account. |
| **4. At least one Stripe product with a price** | Click **Open Stripe dashboard** and add a product with a price. |
| **5. Generate the paywall** | 5.1. Click **Generate**.<br />5.2. Pick up to three plans. Click **Add plan** for each one after the first.<br />5.3. Give each plan a production price, a sandbox price, or both, all in one currency. Fill the same column for every plan — a partly filled column blocks generation. Without sandbox prices, [test emails](mail-testing) hit the production paywall, where test purchases don't work.<br />5.4. Click **Generate paywall**. It takes a few minutes, and the **URL** field fills itself when it's done. |
| **6. Review and publish the paywall in the builder** | Click **Open editor**, then review and publish. Disabled until you've generated the paywall. |

<ZoomImage id="mail-checkout-generation-dialog.webp" width="500px" alt="Generate a paywall dialog with production and sandbox price columns" />

<Callout type="important">
The paywall must be published before it can serve checkout traffic. Unpublished paywalls return an error when users click email checkout links.
</Callout>

Once the paywall is published, click **Save**.
