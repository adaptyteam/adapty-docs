If you change the price in Google Play, Adapty only applies it to existing subscribers after you [move them to the new price](https://developer.android.com/google/play/billing/price-changes#end-legacy).

If you change the price in App Store Connect, you don't need to update anything in Adapty. Apple sends Adapty the price of each App Store renewal. Apps that use the V1 Server Notifications are subject to the [**App Store price increase logic**](general#5-app-store-price-increase-logic) setting.

For financial and accounting purposes, reference the reports in App Store Connect or Google Play Console. To verify renewal prices, pick one product and one day. Compare `revenue_local` in your [Amazon S3](s3-exports) or [Google Cloud Storage](google-cloud-storage) export with the store transaction reports for that product and day. If the prices differ, follow the [troubleshooting algorithm](discrepancies-and-troubleshooting#troubleshooting-algorithm).
