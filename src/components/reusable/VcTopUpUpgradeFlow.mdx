To offer an upgrade to subscribers of the lower tier, show the subscribers of each tier a separate copy of the flow. You need copies because a [condition](onboarding-actions#conditional-actions) in a flow can't evaluate which subscription a player has.

1. [Copy the flow](copy-flows) twice, and edit each copy as follows:
   - **Upgrade copy**: Delete the **Products** element of the subscription. Click the purchase button of the subscription. In its **Purchase** action, select the higher tier from the **Product** dropdown. In the button text, remove any variables of the deleted element, and insert the [`prod_price` and `prod_billing_period`](onboarding-variables#product-variables) variables of the higher tier.
   - **Packs-only copy**: Delete the **Products** element of the subscription and its purchase button.
2. In the `out_of_diamonds` placement, [add an audience for each tier](add-audience-paywall-ab-test). [Set the audience priority](change-audience-priority) in the following order, highest first:

   | Audience | Flow |
   |---|---|
   | Subscribers of the lower tier: a [segment](segments) where **Subscription product** is the lower tier | The upgrade copy |
   | Subscribers of the higher tier: a segment where **Subscription product** is the higher tier | The packs-only copy |
   | **All users** | The original flow |

   After a subscription ends, **Subscription product** keeps its value. To keep former subscribers out of both segments, add the **Calculated subscription state** attribute to each segment. Select the values **Subscribed**, **Active trial**, **Auto-renew off**, and **Grace period**.
