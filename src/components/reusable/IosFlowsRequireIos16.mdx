Starting from SDK 4.4, flows require iOS 16 or later. To build an app that supports iOS 15, mark the types that use flows with `@available(iOS 16.0, *)`, and open the flow inside `if #available(iOS 16.0, *)`. In the `else` branch, show your own content, such as a [custom paywall](ios-implement-paywalls-manually).
