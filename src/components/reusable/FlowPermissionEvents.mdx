The flow reports each permission request to your app's flow analytics callback in two events:

- **`flow_permission_requested`**: The flow sent the request to your app. Its parameters:
  - `permission`: The permission value.
  - `with_outcome`: Whether the action has actions in its outcome sections.
- **`flow_permission_answered`**: Your app answered. Its parameters:
  - `permission`: The permission value.
  - `result`: Your app's answer, `true` for granted.
  - `detail_result`: The status your app passed, if any.
  - `outcome`: The section that the answer selects: `granted` for **When allowed**, `denied` for **When denied**, `response` for **After response**, or `none` for an action without outcome actions.

A `flow_permission_requested` event without a `flow_permission_answered` event after it means that the request got no answer. If `outcome` is `response` where you expected `denied`, make sure that your app passes a status, such as `denied`, with the refusal.
