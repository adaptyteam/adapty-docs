Starting from SDK 4.4, flows require iOS 16 or later. On iOS 15, `AdaptyUI().createFlowView()` and `AdaptyUIFlowPlatformView` report an error with `AdaptyErrorCode.platformNotSupported`. When your app receives this error, show your own content instead of the flow, such as a [custom paywall](flutter-implement-paywalls-manually).
