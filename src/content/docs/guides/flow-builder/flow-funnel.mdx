The **Funnel** tab of a flow shows how users move through its screens and on which screen they leave. [Flow metrics](flow-metrics) report how many users start a flow, how many finish it, and what it earns. The funnel shows the path in between, screen by screen. Use it to find the screen that loses users and fix that screen instead of the whole flow. The [Funnel analysis](analytics-funnels) chart on the Analytics page is a different funnel: it covers installs, paywall views, trials, and payments for the whole app.

The funnel is built from the screen views of a published flow version. The Adapty SDK reports a screen view each time it renders a flow screen, so no code is required.

To open the funnel, on the [**Flows**](https://app.adapty.io/flows) page, click a flow. On its page, select the **Funnel** tab.

<ZoomImage id="flow-funnel-overview.webp" width="700px" alt="Funnel tab of a flow with the Entered, Completed, Drop rate, and Revenue cards, a bar chart with one column per step, and the step table" />

The page has three parts:

- **Summary cards**: **Entered**, **Completed**, **Drop rate**, and **Revenue** for the whole flow.
- **Chart**: One bar per step, with the share of users who reached it, the conversion to the next step, and the drop-off after it.
- **Table**: One row per step: its type, the users who reached it, the users who dropped off on it, and the revenue of its paywall screens.

## Funnel steps

A step is a position in a user's path through the flow, not a position in the Flow & Paywall Builder. The first step is the screen users saw first, the second step is the screen they saw next, and so on. The first step is captioned **Start screen**.

Adapty builds one path per user:

- Each time a user opens the flow, a screen counts once, at its first view. A user who goes back to a screen adds no step.
- If a user opens the flow several times, Adapty keeps the run with the most screens.
- Because each user has one path, every count in the funnel is a count of unique users.

Until the version has data, the table lists its screens in builder order with zero values.

### Branches

Screens of different branches at the same position share one step. The step is titled after the screen most users saw. A badge shows how many screens share it: **N routes** when the step has a paywall screen, **N variants** when it doesn't. Expand the step row to see each screen with its share of the step's users and its share of the step's drop-off. Each paywall screen in a step is labeled **Paywall path N**.

<ZoomImage id="flow-funnel-step-expanded.webp" width="700px" alt="Step table with the Regular paywall step expanded into two screen rows labeled Paywall path 1 and Paywall path 2, each with a View Details button" />

### Completions and drop-offs

A path that ends on a last screen is a completion. A last screen is a screen with no navigation to another screen. A flow can have several of them, for example a success screen and a cancel screen. Each of them is captioned **Last screen**.

A path that ends on any other screen is a drop-off, counted on the step where the path ended. Any drop-off above zero is shown in red.

## Funnel controls

- **Period**: The date range. The funnel includes every run of the flow that started in the period, in full, even if the run continued past the period's end.
- **Flow version**: The version the funnel is computed for. **Default** is the published version. The list offers every version that was published at some point, and the published one is tagged **Published**. Screens and metrics differ between versions, and a version has data from the date it was published. After you change a screen and publish the flow, the new version gets its own funnel from that date. To see whether the change helped, select the previous version and compare the step.
- **Filter metrics by install date**: Counts users who installed the app in the period instead of runs that started in it. See [Filter metrics by install date](#filter-metrics-by-install-date).
- **Filters**: Country, Store, and attribution, the same as in [flow metrics](flow-metrics#filters-and-groups), plus two filters the funnel alone has:
  - **Platform**: Keeps the users who saw the flow on that platform.
  - **Subscription state**: Keeps the users whose current subscription state matches, for example **Never subscribed** or **Active trial**. The state updates about once an hour.
- **Absolute / Relative**: Whether the table shows counts, percentages, or both. The chart always shows both.
- **Download CSV**: Exports one row per step and one row per screen within a step.

## Metrics definitions

Values that don't apply are shown as a dash, for example the revenue of an onboarding screen or the drop rate of a last screen.

### Entered

Unique users who started the flow, meaning they reached the first screen.

### Completed

Unique users who reached a last screen. The card shows the share of entered users and the count.

### Drop rate

Share of users who entered the flow but did not reach a last screen.

### Reached

Unique users who reached the step, as a share of entered users. In a screen row within a step, the share of the step's users who saw that screen.

The chart also shows the conversion to the next step, which is the step's reached users as a share of the previous step's.

### Drop rate per step

Users whose path ended on the step without reaching a last screen, as a share of the users who reached it. In a screen row within a step, the screen's share of the step's drop-off. Hover over the value to see the screen's own drop-off rate.

### Revenue

Gross revenue in USD from initial purchases on the paywall screens of the flow, minus refunds of those purchases. Renewals are not included, unlike the **Revenue** in [flow metrics](flow-metrics#revenue), which counts every transaction after the flow was shown.

A purchase counts on the date it was paid. Each paywall step and screen also shows its **Trials**, the trials started on it, and its **Purchases**, the initial purchases on it minus their refunds.

## Limitations

- **Branches of different lengths shift steps**: A step is a position in the path. When one branch is longer than another, or a screen is skipped, the same screen lands on different steps for different users. The funnel then shows that screen in more than one step.
- **Late conversions are not counted**: A trial that converts after the period ends is not in the period's **Purchases** and **Revenue**. The last days of a period therefore show lower values than they will later.
- **Data is per version**: The funnel covers one flow version at a time, from the date that version was published. To see an earlier period, select the version that was published then.
