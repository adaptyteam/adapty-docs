A hero screen shows an edge-to-edge image or video at the top, with your content on a panel below it. To build one, put all your content on a **Sliding sheet**, with the hero behind it. The sheet covers the bottom part of the screen, and moves up over the hero as users scroll.

## Lay your content over a hero image or video

1. Add the hero as the screen background: in the [screen settings](builder-ui#screen-settings-and-layout), set **Fill** to an [image or a video](paywall-head-picture). The sheet covers the bottom part of the background, so pick an image or video that shows its subject near the top.

    <ZoomImage id="hero-1-background-fill.webp" width="700px" alt="Screen settings with Fill set to Image, and the hero photo filling the screen in the preview area" />

2. Open the **Add element** panel and select **Sliding sheet**.
3. Place the content of the screen in the sliding sheet — drag it in the **Layers** panel.
4. To keep the purchase button in view, add it to a [footer](#keep-the-purchase-button-in-view). The button will stay at the bottom of the screen while the content scrolls.
5. Adjust the sheet's **Start position** to show more of the hero or more of your content when the screen first opens.
6. To see how the sheet moves over the hero, scroll the screen in the [preview area](builder-ui#preview-area-center).
7. Select other models from the [device list](builder-ui#view-controls-bottom-toolbar), and review the area above the sheet. **Start position** is a percentage of the screen height, so that area is shorter on short phones.

## How the sheet scrolls over the hero

**Start position** is the position of the sheet when the screen opens. At the default 55%, the sheet covers the bottom 55% of the screen, and users see the hero in the top 45%.

If all the content is visible when the screen opens, the sheet stays at its start position. Otherwise, users scroll the whole sheet up over the hero. If the content is taller than the screen, the top of the sheet scrolls out of view.

To keep tall content scrollable, in the [screen settings](paywall-layout-and-products#scroll), leave the **Vertical scroll** toggle on. If you turn it off instead, the sheet stays at its start position and cuts off any content that doesn't fit.

While the sheet moves, users see the following:

- The sheet slides up over the elements outside it, such as a logo or a headline at the top of the screen. These elements move up too, but more slowly, which gives the screen a sense of depth (parallax). On Android, parallax is available in native Android and React Native apps with Adapty SDK v4.3.0 or later. In other Android apps — or older SDK versions — the effect is not visible.
- On iOS, when users pull the sheet below its start position, the elements outside the sheet stretch.
- The screen background doesn't move.

### Add parallax to the hero

To give the hero the parallax effect, add it as an image or video element outside the sheet, instead of the screen background.

The element doesn't reach the screen edges by default. The screen padding insets the element on every side, and with **Safe area** on, it starts below the status bar.

1. Open the **Add element** panel and drag an **Image** or **Video** element onto the area above the sheet.
2. To make the element reach down to the sheet, set its **Width** and **Height** to **Fill**. Keep **Content mode** (**Aspect** on a video) at **Fill**.
3. To make the element reach the left and right edges of the screen, open the [screen settings](builder-ui#screen-settings-and-layout). Under **Spacing**, set the left and right padding to 0. If the sheet has square top corners, set the bottom padding to 0 too, so the element reaches the sheet.
4. To make the element reach the top edge of the screen, under the status bar, keep it as the first element outside the sheet. Then click the element, and in the **Position** section, select the **Ignore safe area** checkbox.

## Place elements over the hero

To place a logo or a headline over a background hero, drag the element onto the area above the sheet. Elements outside the sheet stack from the top of the screen, and whatever doesn't fit in that area is cut off. To place them over an [image or video element](#add-parallax-to-the-hero), set their **Position** to **Absolute**.

To keep a close button in view while users scroll, add it directly to the screen and set its **Position** to **Fixed**. You can't select **Fixed** for an element inside the sheet.

:::important
On Android, elements outside the sheet with **Relative** or **Absolute** position only respond to taps in the following apps:

- Native Android apps with Adapty SDK v4.2.1 or later
- React Native apps with Adapty SDK v4.2.2 or later

In other Android apps, keep buttons and other tappable elements inside the sheet.
:::

## Keep the purchase button in view

To keep the purchase button and legal links visible while users scroll, put them in a [footer](builder-containers#footer). The footer goes inside the sheet.

The footer stays at the bottom of the screen, over the sheet. Users can scroll all of the sheet content into view above the footer.

A footer at the screen root looks the same as one in the sheet.

## Style the sheet

You style the sheet like any other container. Padding and corners work differently:

- **Screen padding**: The screen padding only applies to elements outside the sheet, so the sheet spans the screen from edge to edge.
- **Element padding**: The **Padding** of the sheet keeps its content off the screen edges.
- **Corners**: Rounded top corners lift the sheet above its start position by the corner radius, so the hero shows in them. A sheet with a video fill doesn't lift, so its corners show the screen background. A see-through sheet only lifts over a screen background of one solid color.

## Sliding sheet vs. Bottom Sheet

Both panels occupy the bottom part of the screen. Pick the one that matches what you need:

- **Use a Sliding sheet** to lay the main content of the screen over a hero. The sheet is visible when the screen opens, and moves only when the user scrolls.
- **Use a [Bottom Sheet](builder-containers#bottom-sheet)** for content that appears on demand, such as a plan list behind a **Show all plans** link. You reveal it with a **Show** action.
