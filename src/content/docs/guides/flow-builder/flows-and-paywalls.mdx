A paywall screen shows your products and allows the user to purchase them. Adapty gives you two ways to build it:

- **Flows & paywalls**: You design the screens in the [Flow & Paywall Builder](adapty-flow-builder), and the Adapty SDK renders them natively in your app. Build a single paywall, or a whole onboarding that ends in one.
- **Custom paywall**: You build the screen in your own app code and save the products it sells in Adapty as a [custom paywall](paywalls). Adapty runs the purchase.

## Compare flows & paywalls with custom paywalls

| | Flows & paywalls | Custom paywalls |
|---|---|---|
| **Who builds the UI** | You, in a visual no-code editor. The SDK renders it natively, without web views. | Your developers, in your app code. |
| **How to start** | A [template](paywall-builder-templates), a blank canvas, or a [Figma import](import-from-figma). | Your existing screen and design system. |
| **Build with AI in the dashboard** | The [AI Editor](flow-ai-editor) restyles, rewrites, or rearranges a screen from a plain-language request or a reference screenshot. | Not available. |
| **Build with your AI coding tool** | The [flow-generator skill](flow-generator-skill) builds a flow or paywall from a description or a screenshot, and edits copy, locales, and screens. The [flow-audit skill](flow-audit-skill) reviews it for problems before you publish. | The [SDK integration skill](adapty-sdk-integration-skill) creates the custom paywall through the [Developer CLI](developer-cli-reference#adapty-paywalls-create) and writes the purchase code in your app. |
| **Change without an app release** | Design, copy, screens, logic, and products. | Products, their order, offers, and the [remote config](customize-paywall-with-remote-config) values your code reads. Layout changes need an app release. |
| **Multi-screen journeys** | Onboarding screens, [quizzes](onboarding-quizzes), and [branching](onboarding-navigation-branching) before the paywall, with user answers [passed to your app](ios-flow-input). | Whatever your code builds. |
| **Localization** | Per-screen [locales](add-paywall-locale-in-adapty-paywall-builder) in the builder. | Per-locale [remote config](add-remote-config-locale), with AI translation in the dashboard. |
| **A/B tests** | Between flows or paywalls on a flow placement. | Between custom paywalls on a paywall placement. |
| **Analytics** | [Flow metrics](flow-metrics), with revenue attributed to each screen. Views are tracked automatically. | [Custom paywall metrics](paywall-metrics). Your code reports each view to Adapty. |
| **Web purchases** | Yes, with a [web paywall](web-paywall). | Yes, with a [web paywall](web-paywall). |
| **Placement type** | [Flow placement](create-placement). | [Paywall placement](create-placement). |
| **Adapty SDK** | v4.0 or later. | Any version. |

A paywall from the Flow & Paywall Builder and a custom paywall can't share one A/B test, because each needs its own placement type. To compare them, [roll out the builder paywall gradually](flow-gradual-rollout) on its own placement.

## Which one to choose

- **Choose flows & paywalls** when you want to design, test, and change the purchase screen without writing UI code or shipping an app release, or when the paywall is part of an onboarding.
- **Choose a custom paywall** when you want to build the screen in your own code — for example, to reuse your app's components.

Both live in **Flows & Paywalls** in the Adapty main menu: flows & paywalls on the **Flow & Paywall builder** tab, custom paywalls on the **Custom Paywalls** tab.

Paywalls built in the [old Paywall Builder](adapty-paywall-builder) stay editable on the **Custom Paywalls & Legacy** tab, but you can't create new ones there. We recommend [migrating them to the Flow & Paywall Builder](migrate-to-flows).
