A flow can continue after a paywall screen — resume the onboarding, show the new subscribers a thank-you screen, or welcome back those who restore an earlier purchase.

You set outcomes in the action settings, one for each purchase condition. An outcome without actions keeps users on the paywall screen.

- The **Purchase** action has the following conditions: **When succeeded**, **When failed**, **When cancelled by user**, and **When pending**.
- The **Restore purchases** action has two conditions: **When restore completed** and **When restore failed**. 


## Before you start

Make sure that:

- Your app uses Adapty SDK v4.0 or later.
- The flow has a paywall screen. To build one, follow the [Create a basic paywall screen](basic-paywall-screen) recipe.
- An element on the paywall screen, such as a button, has a [**Purchase** action](paywall-product-block#trigger-a-purchase).
- The flow stays open after a purchase or a purchase restoration. Outcome actions only run while the flow is open, and your app's <InlineTooltip tooltip="purchase and restoration callbacks">[iOS](ios-handling-events), [Android](android-handling-events), [React Native](react-native-handling-events-1), [Flutter](flutter-handling-events), [Unity](unity-handling-events), [Kotlin Multiplatform](kmp-handling-events), and [Capacitor](capacitor-handling-events)</InlineTooltip> run before them. If a callback closes the flow, the outcome actions never run.
- On Android, the app sets an `AdaptyFlowEventListener` for the flow, and the callbacks of that listener don't throw errors. Otherwise, outcome actions don't run.

## Add actions for a result

:::important
The flow can only respond to **In-App Store** purchases. It doesn't receive the result of a **Web Payment** purchase.
:::

1. Select the element that has the **Purchase** or **Restore purchases** action.
2. Click the **Purchase** or **Restore purchases** action.
3. For the purchase or restoration result you need, such as **When succeeded**, click <Inline id="plus.svg" alt="Add action" />.
4. From the **Action** dropdown, select the action to run instead of **Do nothing**.

:::note
A navigation or **Close Flow** action that follows **Purchase** or **Restore purchases** in the same trigger runs right away, before the result. Add it as an outcome action instead.
:::

To see the outcome actions in your app, [make a sandbox purchase](test-purchases-in-sandbox).

:::tip
To handle purchase results in your app code instead, for example to open a native screen, use the purchase and restoration callbacks of the Adapty SDK. Each change to that code needs an app release. If your app opens a second flow after a purchase, you can move its screens into the flow that contains the paywall.
:::

### Show the next screen after a successful purchase

To continue the onboarding or show a thank-you screen after a successful purchase, pair the **When succeeded** outcome with a [**Navigate to Screen**](onboarding-actions#navigate-to-screen) action, and select that screen as the destination.

### Close the flow after a successful purchase

To end the flow after a successful purchase, add [**Close Flow**](onboarding-actions#close-flow) for **When succeeded** instead of a navigation. The flow then closes only when the purchase succeeds, so your app code doesn't need to close it.

### Respond to a failed, canceled, or pending purchase

To show an alert or a screen after a failed, canceled, or pending purchase, add actions for these outcomes of the **Purchase** action:

- **When failed**: An error stopped the purchase. For example, [show an alert](onboarding-actions#show-alert) that asks the user to try again later.
- **When cancelled by user**: The user canceled the purchase. For example, show a screen with another offer.
- **When pending**: The purchase waits for approval, for example while the bank confirms the payment, or while a parent approves it through Ask to Buy on iOS. The flow doesn't respond when the bank or the parent approves the purchase, so show a screen that explains the wait.

### Respond to a purchase restoration

The [**Restore purchases**](paywall-product-block#restore-purchases) action has two outcomes: **When restore completed** and **When restore failed**.

**When restore completed** means that the restoration finished without an error, not that the user has access. You can't verify access levels in a flow. To show content that depends on access, add **Close Flow** for **When restore completed**, and verify the [access level](access-level) in the app.
