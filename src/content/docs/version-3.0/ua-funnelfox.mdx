[FunnelFox](https://funnelfox.com) is a web2app funnel platform, which allows you to acquire and charge users outside the App Store — bypassing its fee and other restrictions.

Configure this integration to send FunnelFox purchase data to Adapty. Otherwise, Adapty knows what you spent on ads — but cannot attribute the resulting purchases to your campaigns.

You don't need to add the [Adapty web pixel](ua-web-pixel) — FunnelFox reports purchases to Adapty directly. Instead, add your FunnelFox **Project ID** to Adapty Attribution. In Meta or TikTok, add Adapty's tracking parameters to your ads. Then turn on campaign attribution in FunnelFox. This article will walk you through the process.

## How it works

When a user completes a purchase in your FunnelFox funnel, FunnelFox sends the transaction event to Adapty Attribution. Adapty uses the **Project ID** to identify which app the transaction belongs to. The event is then stored and appears in your Adapty Attribution analytics.

Once the integration is running, each transaction in Adapty includes:

- **Subscription lifecycle event**: started, renewed, cancelled, trial converted, refunded, and more
- **Attribution channel**: Facebook, TikTok, Google, or organic. Adapty determines it from the click ID, which the ad networks add on their own — you don't configure it.
- **Attribution data**: campaign, ad set, and ad identifiers; UTM parameters; platform click IDs (fbclid, ttclid, gclid). The campaign, ad set, and ad arrive only if your ads carry Adapty's [tracking parameters](#step-3-tag-your-ad-urls).
- **Funnel and experiment data**: FunnelFox funnel name and experiment name, so you can compare A/B test variants
- **Cohort date**: the date of the first trial start or purchase. Web purchases have no app install event, so Adapty cohorts them by that date instead.

## Configure integration

### Step 1. Get your Project ID in FunnelFox

1. In your FunnelFox dashboard, click **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. In the **Project info** section, copy the **ID** value.

<ZoomImage id="ff-project-id.webp" width="500px" />

### Step 2. Add the project in Adapty Attribution

1. In Adapty Attribution, go to [**Integrations > FunnelFox**](https://app.adapty.io/ua/integrations/funnelfox).
2. Paste the Project ID you copied from FunnelFox.
3. Click **Save**.

To connect additional FunnelFox projects, click **Add project** and repeat this step for each one.

<ZoomImage id="ua-funnelfox.webp" width="500px" />

### Step 3. Tag your ad URLs

FunnelFox only sends the campaign and ad information if it reaches your landing page URL. It captures that information when the visitor first lands, not when they reach checkout. See [Tag your ad URLs for web funnels](ua-tag-ad-urls).

### Step 4. Turn on campaign attribution in FunnelFox

Don't postpone this step. This setting is off by default. Until you turn it on, FunnelFox sends no transactions to Adapty Attribution, and doesn't backfill them after the fact.

1. In FunnelFox, go to [**Integrations > Adapty**](https://app.funnelfox.com/integrations/adapty).
2. Turn on **Campaign attribution**.

Turn this on for every FunnelFox project you connected.

Your FunnelFox project must belong to an organization, and your ad accounts must be connected in Adapty.

For the events FunnelFox sends through this channel, see [Adapty integration](https://funnelfox.com/docs/integrations/subscription-management/adapty#campaign-attribution) in the FunnelFox docs.
