[Retention Messaging](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/retentionmessaging) is an Apple feature that shows subscribers a custom message on the iOS **Cancel Subscription** screen, right before they confirm. Use it to cut subscription churn by giving people a reason — or an offer — to stay.

Adapty connects to Apple's Retention Messaging API, so Apple requests the right message in real time whenever a subscriber taps **Cancel Subscription**. Adapty runs that endpoint for you: you write, localize, and test messages in the Adapty Dashboard, and Adapty answers Apple automatically. No backend to build, no app release.

There are two message types. A *default* message is plain copy, and serves as the fallback whenever a promo message can't be shown. A *promo* message adds a concrete incentive on top: a discount, a free trial, or a switch to a more fitting plan. Apple shows each subscriber the message that matches their product and language.

## Quickstart

Before you start, you need:

- An [App Store Connect API key](app-store-connection-configuration) in Adapty.
- [Apple's approval for your app](retention-messaging-setup#request-access-from-apple). Apple's review takes up to a month, so submit the request early.
- At least one [product](create-product) for your messages to cover.

Then:

1. [Connect Adapty to the Retention Messaging API in sandbox mode](retention-messaging-setup).
2. Purchase your longest-duration product with a sandbox account or through TestFlight, and keep the subscription active.
3. [Pass Apple's performance test](retention-messaging-setup#pass-the-performance-test). It can take up to an hour.
4. Switch to production mode.
5. [Create your production messages](retention-messaging-create) — first, default messages, then promo messages — and submit each for Apple's review.
6. [Track how each message performs](retention-messaging-analytics) once Apple approves it.
