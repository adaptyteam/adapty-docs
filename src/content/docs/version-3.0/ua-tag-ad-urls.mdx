Adapty Attribution reads the campaign, ad set, and ad from your landing page URL. The ad networks append their own click ID (`fbclid` or `ttclid`) automatically, which identifies the network but not the campaign, ad set, or ad. To attribute purchases down to the exact ad, add the tracking parameters to your ads.

Tag your ad URLs whichever way your funnel runs — with the [Adapty web pixel](ua-web-pixel) on your own funnel, or on [FunnelFox](ua-funnelfox).

## Before you start

Connect [Meta Ads](ua-facebook) or [TikTok for Business](ua-tiktok) to Adapty Attribution and add a campaign configuration. Adapty generates the ad URL parameters per campaign configuration, so you need one to copy them from.

## Tag your ads

1. In the **Web funnel** section of the campaign configuration, copy the **Ad URL parameters** field value.

<ZoomImage id="ua-web-pixel-url-params.webp" width="700px" alt="Ad URL parameters field in the Web funnel section of a campaign configuration in Adapty Attribution" />

2. In the ad network, paste the value at the **ad** level:
   - **Meta**: Into the ad's **URL parameters** field.
   - **TikTok**: Under the ad's destination URL, into **Build URL parameters**.

The value contains network macros. The network replaces them with the real campaign, ad set, and ad values at click time.

The parameters don't include `utm_*` values, so you can keep your own UTM scheme for your own analytics — Adapty doesn't require it.
