Tag your ad URLs when people buy on the web after they click your ad, for example in a web funnel. If your ad goes straight to the App Store or Google Play, use a [tracking link](ua-tracking-links) as its destination instead — the link carries the campaign data on its own, with nothing to tag.

Adapty Attribution reads the campaign, ad set, and ad from your landing page URL. The ad networks append their own click ID (`fbclid`, `ttclid`, or `gclid`) automatically, which identifies the network but not the campaign, ad set, or ad. To attribute purchases down to the exact ad, add the tracking parameters to your ads.

The steps are the same whichever way your funnel runs — with the [Adapty web pixel](ua-web-pixel) on your own funnel, or on [FunnelFox](ua-funnelfox).

## Before you start

Connect [Meta Ads](ua-facebook), [TikTok for Business](ua-tiktok), or [Google Ads](ua-google-ads) to Adapty Attribution and add a campaign configuration. Adapty generates the ad URL parameters per campaign configuration, so you need one to copy them from.

## Tag your ads

1. In the **Web funnel** section of the campaign configuration, copy the **Ad URL parameters** field value.

<ZoomImage id="ua-web-pixel-url-params.webp" width="700px" alt="Ad URL parameters field in the Web funnel section of a campaign configuration in Adapty Attribution" />

2. In the ad network, paste the value:
   - **Meta**: Into the ad's **URL parameters** field.
   - **TikTok**: Under the ad's destination URL, into **Build URL parameters**.
   - **Google Ads**: In the campaign settings, into **Campaign URL options > Final URL suffix**. Auto-tagging must also be on in your Google Ads account settings.

The value contains network macros. The network replaces them with the real campaign, ad set, and ad values at click time.

Keep your landing page or funnel URL in the ad's destination field (**Website URL** in Meta, **Destination URL** in TikTok, **Final URL** in Google Ads) without the parameters. The network appends them to that URL at click time, so you can change the destination URL later without tagging the ad again.

The parameters don't include `utm_*` values, so you can keep your own UTM scheme for your own analytics — Adapty doesn't require it.
