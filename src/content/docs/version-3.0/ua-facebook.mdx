With the Meta integration of Adapty Attribution, you can track and optimize your campaigns on Facebook, Instagram, Messenger, and Audience Network.

:::tip
See our [guide on setting up ads in Meta Ads Manager](meta-create-campaign).
:::

## Step 1. Connect your Facebook account

To connect Meta Ads to Adapty Attribution, go to **Integrations > Meta** from the left sidebar. You have two options:

- **Continue with Facebook**: Connects via OAuth. Use this if you sign in to Meta Ads Manager with your personal or business Facebook account.
- **Add system token**: Connects using a permanent system user token. Use this if your organization manages ad accounts through a Meta Business system user.

<Tabs>
<TabItem value="oauth" label="Continue with Facebook">

:::important
Ensure your Facebook account has access to campaigns and pixels you need.
:::

1. Click **Continue with Facebook**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-connect-meta.webp" width="700px" />

2. Sign in using your Facebook account and click **Continue**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-fb-sign.webp" width="500px" />

3. Review the requested permissions and click **Save**.

:::note
Meta can disconnect the integration when the connected Facebook account hits a security checkpoint, and spend then stops arriving in Adapty Attribution. To restore the connection, log in to [facebook.com](https://www.facebook.com), complete the checkpoint, and connect again in **Integrations > Meta**.
:::

</TabItem>
<TabItem value="system" label="Add system token">

Generate a [system user access token](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/collaborative-ads/managed-partner-ads/api-guide/prerequisites/generate-access-token-system-user) in Meta Business Settings, then add it to Adapty Attribution.

:::important
Before you start, you need a [system user](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/503306463479099) in your Meta Business portfolio, with the ad accounts you want to track already assigned to it. You also need an app added to the portfolio — you select this app when you generate the token.
:::

**In Meta Business Settings, generate the token:**

1. Go to **Business Settings**.
2. Under **Users**, select **System users**.
3. Select your system user, then click **Generate new token**.
4. Select your app from the dropdown.
5. In the permissions list, enable `ads_read`. This is the only permission Adapty Attribution needs to read your campaign and ad data.
6. Click **Generate token**.
7. Copy the token and store it securely. Meta shows it only once.

:::note
The **Token expiration** setting controls how long the connection stays active. A token with an expiration date must be regenerated and reconnected before it expires, or attribution stops. A token with no expiration avoids this, but is a long-lived credential. Store it securely, and revoke it if it is ever exposed.
:::

**In Adapty Attribution, add the token:**

1. Click **Add system token**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-add-meta-system-token.webp" width="700px" alt="Add system token button on the Meta integration page in Adapty Attribution" />

2. Paste the token and click **Connect**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-connect-meta-system-token.webp" width="700px" alt="System token input field on the Meta integration page in Adapty Attribution" />

After that, Adapty Attribution adds your ad accounts. You can proceed with adding campaigns.

If you create or gain access to a new ad account, connect again in **Integrations** > **Meta**.

## Step 2. Add campaigns

A single campaign can cover the App Store, Google Play, and your web funnel at once. Two pieces make the connection work:

- **Tracking link**: Goes into your ad. When a user taps the ad, Adapty records the click, sends the user on to the destination, and later attributes the install and purchases to that ad. Each destination gets its own link.
- **Pixel**: Selected in the campaign, from the pixels in your Meta account. Carries those conversions back to Meta, so Meta can optimize the campaign on real purchases. One pixel can serve all three destinations, or each destination can have its own.

To add a new Meta campaign to Adapty Attribution:

1. Switch to the app that you want to advertise.

2. Open the **Web Campaigns** tab and click **Create configuration**. This configuration only counts the installs of the app that you switched to.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-new-campaign.webp" width="500px" />

3. In the **General** tab, expand the **iOS** and/or **Android** section and paste App Store and/or Google Play application URLs.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-url.webp" width="900px" />

4. Copy the **Click link** field value. Then, in Meta Ads Manager, open your ad and split the link between the **Website URL** and **URL parameters** fields, as described in [Create ads](meta-create-campaign#step-5-create-ads). This will allow Adapty to connect installs and purchases to ads in Meta. Adapty only connects installs to your ad when users go straight from it to the store through this link.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-lnk.webp" width="900px" />

   Put the link into ads of campaigns with the **Sales** objective. Adapty Attribution doesn't track **App promotion** campaigns: it shows their spend, but no installs or revenue.

5. (Optional) To send the conversion events back to Meta, associate your Meta pixels with this configuration:
   1. From the **Pixel** dropdown, select the pixel that your ad set uses as its dataset. If you don't have a pixel yet, [create one in Meta](meta-create-campaign#step-2-add-meta-pixel).
   2. To verify the connection, click **Send test event**.

6. If your ads lead to a web funnel, expand the **Web funnel** section of the configuration:
   1. (Optional) From the **Pixel** dropdown, select the pixel for web purchases. Meta only learns about purchases on your funnel through this pixel.
   2. To install the [Adapty web pixel](ua-web-pixel), paste the **Pixel snippet** on every page of your funnel.
   3. To [tag your ad URLs](ua-tag-ad-urls), copy the **Ad URL parameters**.

   If your funnel runs on [FunnelFox](ua-funnelfox), skip the snippet. Adapty doesn't send FunnelFox purchases to Meta. To send them, set up the [Meta Pixel integration](https://funnelfox.com/docs/integrations/tracking/meta-pixel) in FunnelFox.

To set up your pixels correctly:

- **Match the pixels to your campaigns**: If one campaign covers the App Store, Google Play, and your web funnel, select the same pixel in all three sections. If you run a separate campaign for each, select a separate pixel for each, so that every campaign optimizes on its own purchases. The **Web Campaigns** list shows the pixels of each configuration in the **Pixel (iOS)**, **Pixel (Android)**, and **Pixel (Web)** columns.
- **Edit the existing configuration to switch pixels**: Running ads keep the click link of their configuration, so their conversions go to that configuration's pixels. To switch the pixel of running ads, select another pixel in that configuration. A new configuration doesn't change where their conversions go.
- **Fill in the Event source URL**: If you connected your pixel to your website in Meta, enter the website's address in the **Event source URL** field of the configuration. Otherwise, Meta may mark the conversions from Adapty as coming from an unknown website. This field doesn't change the address of your tracking links.

## Step 3. Map events

Adapty sends the events that you select, such as started trials, to the pixels of the configuration. Meta uses these events to find people who are likely to start a trial or buy a subscription.

To select the events, in the **Events mapping** section of the configuration:

1. Turn on each event that Adapty should send to Meta.
2. For each event, select a Meta event from the dropdown, or type a custom one. By default, Adapty selects Meta's standard events. **Install** can only be sent as **Lead**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Additional configuration

### Additional parameters

The **Additional parameter** field allows you to add custom data points for analysis outside Adapty. This is useful when you need to pass specific campaign or user data to external analytics tools or attribution partners.

In the **Additional parameter** field, enter any custom data you want to include with your attribution tracking. The additional parameter will be included in all attribution data sent to Meta and can be used for advanced campaign analysis and optimization.

For example, if you're running multiple variations of the same campaign, you could add `variant=A` or `variant=B` to distinguish between different creative approaches.

:::important
Additional parameters change the **Click link** you paste in Meta Ads Manager. If you have already copied this link there and added a custom parameter later, ensure you copy and paste an updated click link that contains the custom parameter.
:::

### Settings

The **Settings** tab of a configuration controls how Adapty matches installs to clicks on your Meta ads. Adapty uses one of two methods:

| Method | Installs | How Adapty credits the install | Time limit between the click and the install |
|---|---|---|---|
| Match by click ID | Android installs with a click ID | To the click with the ID that Google Play passes to your app | 168 hours (7 days) by default, up to 720 hours |
| Probabilistic match | iOS installs, and Android installs without a click ID | To the most likely recent click from the same IP address range and operating system | 6 hours by default, up to 24 hours |

For a probabilistic match, Adapty selects the click that scores best on time since the click, device language, and country. Adapty doesn't use the IDFA or the Google Advertising ID, so probabilistic matching covers iOS users who don't allow tracking.

Adapty can't match an install probabilistically if the device switches networks between the click and the install, for example from Wi-Fi to cellular. On networks that many devices share, such as mobile carrier networks, Adapty matches fewer installs probabilistically.

To change the time limits, in the **New user attribution** section of the **Settings** tab, enter each limit in hours:

- **Deterministic Matching Window**: For a match by click ID.
- **Probabilistic Matching Window**: For a probabilistic match. A longer limit matches more installs, but more of those matches are wrong. For campaigns with high click volumes, reduce it to 1–2 hours. If you set more than 6 hours, clicks more than 6 hours before the install must also match the device model and OS version of the install.

Keep the switches of both fields turned on. Without probabilistic matching, Adapty can't match iOS installs from the links of this configuration.

<ZoomImage id="ua-meta-attribution-settings.webp" width="900px" alt="New user attribution section on the Settings tab, with the deterministic and probabilistic matching windows and their on/off switches" />

### Revenue override

By default, Adapty sends the **Trial started** event to Meta without a revenue value, because no payment happens when a trial starts. With a revenue override, Adapty sends a value that you set: a percentage of the subscription price. For example, at 30%, Adapty sends 30% of the subscription price. **Trial converted** and **Subscription started** always have their actual revenue.

A revenue override changes the value that Meta receives for a trial. A [qualified trial](#qualified-trial) changes when Meta receives it. You can use both for the same event.

To report revenue to Meta for started trials:

1. In the **Events mapping** section, turn on the **Trial started** event. The **Revenue override** section appears.
2. In the **Revenue override** section, click **Add event**.
3. Select the Meta event that you mapped to **Trial started**, such as **StartTrial**.
4. Enter the percentage of the subscription price to send, from 0 to 100.
5. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="revenue-override.webp" width="500px" />

### Qualified trial

:::important
Qualified trial reports require [App Store Server Notifications](enable-app-store-server-notifications) for iOS and [Real-time Developer Notifications](enable-real-time-developer-notifications-rtdn) for Android. The App Store and Google Play report trial cancellations to Adapty through these channels.
:::

By default, Adapty sends each started trial to Meta immediately, even if the user cancels it soon after. With a qualified trial, Meta only counts the trials that users keep — if the autorenewal isn't disabled a set number of hours after the trial start. Adapty sends the event with the original trial start time, so Meta reports it on the day the trial started. If the event also has a revenue override, Adapty sends it with the overridden revenue.

To only send Meta the trials that users keep:

1. In the **Events mapping** section, turn on the **Trial started** event.
2. In the **Qualified trial** section, click **Add event**.
3. Select the Meta event that you mapped to **Trial started**, such as **StartTrial**.
4. Enter the number of hours to wait, from 1 to 24. Adapty sends the event about 45 minutes after the wait ends.
5. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="qualified-trial.webp" width="700px" alt="Qualified trial section with the StartTrial target event held for two hours in a campaign configuration" />

If the user turns auto-renewal back on before the wait ends, Adapty sends the event as planned. Qualified trials only apply to trials from the App Store and Google Play.

#### Qualified trials vs. the Trial still active event

Adapty has two ways to only report the trials that users keep. For Meta, use a qualified trial: Adapty Attribution can't send the [Trial still active](events#synthetic-events) event to Meta. If another integration, such as AppsFlyer, also sends trials to Meta, Meta receives the same trial more than once.

| | Qualified trial | **Trial still active** event |
|---|---|---|
| Where you set it up | In Adapty Attribution, in a Meta, TikTok, or Google Ads configuration | In Adapty, in integrations such as AppsFlyer, Adjust, Firebase, or the webhook |
| What it does | Delays the **Trial started** event | Adds a separate event after **Trial started** |
| Wait time | 1 to 24 hours | 1 hour to 90 days |
| If the user turns off auto-renewal before the wait ends | Adapty doesn't send the event | Adapty doesn't send the event |
| Sent to Meta by Adapty Attribution | Yes | No |

### Predicted LTV

Meta's value optimization bids on what a conversion is worth, but on its own it sees only the payment being reported — so a monthly trial and a yearly subscription look almost alike at bid time. Use the **Predicted LTV** section to send Meta a [predicted lifetime value](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/conversions-api/guides/predicted-lifetime-value) alongside the conversion, so Meta bids on what the user is likely to be worth over the horizon you choose.

Adapty produces the prediction from your own app's realized revenue, grouped by country, store, device, and locale, and attaches it to a conversion it has already sent.

Predicted LTV unlocks for an app once its data can carry a meaningful prediction. Adapty recalculates this daily and shows the app's current position in the **Predicted LTV** section. **Trial started** and **Subscription started** are measured separately, and either one unlocks the app by clearing both bars:

- At least 100 Meta-attributed conversions of that event in each of the last 4 full weeks.
- At least 5 distinct predicted values, with the highest at least 3× the lowest.

Meta adds two requirements that Adapty can't verify for you: the dataset must be out of Core Setup, and the ad account must have a validated value model in use.

To send predicted values for a campaign:

1. In **Events mapping**, enable **Trial started** or **Subscription started** and map it to a Meta event.
2. In **Predicted LTV**, turn on **Send predicted LTV to Meta**.
3. In **Anchor event**, select the event you mapped in step 1.
4. In **Prediction horizon**, select how far ahead the value looks: 30, 90, 180, or 365 days.
5. In **Target event**, select the Meta event the predicted value attaches to. The list shows only the events this campaign maps the anchor to. Meta accepts predicted values on **Purchase**, **Subscribe**, **StartTrial**, **CompleteRegistration**, **AddPaymentInfo**, and custom events only.
6. Click **Save**.
7. To let Meta use the values, in Events Manager, open your dataset and select the target event in **Value and Currency Metrics**. Click **Set up pLTV** and choose **After [Event Name] events**, because Adapty delivers the value after the conversion, not with it.

<ZoomImage id="ua-meta-pltv.webp" width="900px" alt="Predicted LTV section with the toggle on, Trial started as the anchor event, a 180-day horizon, and StartTrial as the target event" />

:::warning
Meta learns from the anchor event and the horizon you picked. Changing either one on a live setup restarts Meta's learning phase, and ROAS before and after the change is not comparable. Meta also calculates ROAS differently for each optimization setting, so compare a pLTV campaign only with other pLTV campaigns.
:::

Meta accepts one anchor event and one horizon per dataset, so campaigns sharing a pixel have to send the same pair. Saving a campaign whose pair disagrees with another enabled campaign on that pixel is rejected, and the error names the campaign to align with.

Adapty prepares the predicted value when it sends the anchor conversion, then delivers the value 24 hours later — inside Meta's 7-day window for attaching a value to a conversion. Three cases change what Meta receives:

- When the anchor event is held by [Qualified trial](#qualified-trial), the 24 hours start once the trial event reaches Meta. A trial cancelled before it qualifies sends neither the conversion nor a predicted value.
- When Adapty has no prediction for that user's group — a country you started buying in recently, for example — the conversion still goes out, without a predicted value.
- Turning off **Send predicted LTV to Meta** also drops the predicted values still waiting to be delivered.

Adapty sends predicted values in USD.

Once Events Manager shows no open issues for the integration, Meta needs about two weeks to train its model before you start the first pLTV campaign. To read the results, in Ads Manager, add the pLTV column for the target event, such as **StartTrial pLTV**, through **Columns > Customize columns > Results ROAS**. Adapty delivers each value a day after the conversion, so wait one day after a campaign ends before reading its final numbers.

### Send all events

By default, Adapty only reports the events of users who came from one of your Meta campaigns. The **Send all events** option reports every install and transaction instead, including those from organic and non-attributed users. Purchases from FunnelFox funnels are the exception: Adapty only uses them in Adapty Attribution analytics. To send them to Meta, set up the [Meta Pixel integration](https://funnelfox.com/docs/integrations/tracking/meta-pixel) in FunnelFox.

We don't recommend it unless your campaigns send Meta too few conversions to optimize on, because mixing organic and non-attributed events with your campaign events makes Meta's optimization less precise.

If you decide to turn it on, in the configuration settings select **Send all events (forward organic/non-attributed events to this pixel)** and click **Save**.

## Read Meta Ads data correctly in Adapty Attribution

To compare Meta spend with your installs and revenue in [Adapty Attribution analytics](ua-analytics):

- **Expect recent spend to change**: Meta keeps adjusting spend, impressions, and clicks for recent days. Every 4 hours, Adapty refreshes these numbers for the last 3 days, including today.
- **Expect daily spend and revenue to use different time zones**: Adapty Attribution shows Meta spend by day in the time zone of your ad account. It shows installs and revenue by day in UTC. If the two time zones differ, daily spend and daily revenue cover different hours. To show installs and revenue in another time zone, contact [Adapty support](mailto:support@adapty.io).
