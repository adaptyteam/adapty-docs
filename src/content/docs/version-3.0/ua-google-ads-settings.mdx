This article covers the Adapty Attribution settings used by every Google Ads integration, whether you advertise an Android app, an iOS app, or a web funnel.

## Required settings

### Set account permissions

You use two kinds of accounts with Google Ads:

- **Google account**: Your Google login, such as you@appname.com. It can be authorized to open and/or manage several Advertising accounts.
- **Advertising account**: An account in Google Ads with its own campaigns, billing, and customer ID, such as 123-456-7890. It can be viewed and managed by multiple Google accounts.

What your **Google account** can do with each **Advertising account** depends on the permissions you set. So, before you connect the Google account to Adapty, you need to set the correct permissions:

- ✅ **Standard**: The Google account can make changes in the advertising account, including creating conversion actions. This is the minimum access level for Adapty.
- ✅ **Admin**: The Google account can do everything that **Standard** allows, plus manage the access of other Google accounts.
- ❌ **Read-only**: The Google account can only view the advertising account. Adapty can't create conversion actions, so it can't save the configuration.
- ❌ **Billing** and **Email-only**: The Google account has no access to the campaigns.

Give this access to each Google account that you connect:

1. In Google Ads, click the **Admin** icon and select **Access and security**.
2. Find the email address of the Google account. In the **Access level** column, select **Standard** or **Admin**.
3. If the Google account isn't on the list, click the plus button. Enter its email address, select **Standard** or **Admin**, and click **Send invitation**.

### Connect your Google account

:::note
If you connected your Google account before September 11, 2026, reconnect it. Otherwise, Adapty can't send conversions to Google Ads.
:::

Connect every Google account that you use with Google Ads. Adapty uses this access to:

- List your advertising accounts. This is necessary to specify a particular advertising account during campaign configuration.
- Send conversions tracked by Adapty to these advertising accounts.
- Refresh the spend, impressions, and clicks of your campaigns every 4 hours.

To connect a Google account:

1. In Adapty Attribution, go to **Integrations** from the left sidebar and select **Google Ads**.
2. Click **Sign in with Google**.

     <ZoomImage id="ua-sign-in-google-ads.webp" width="700px" alt="Accounts tab of the Google Ads integration in Adapty Attribution with the Sign in with Google button" />

3. Select the Google account and click **Continue**.
4. Select all the requested permissions and click **Continue**.

Adapty connects every advertising account that the Google account can open, including the accounts under a manager account. Adapty doesn't connect closed accounts or accounts that aren't enabled yet.

If you sign in again with a Google account that's already connected, Adapty updates its connection.

### Map events

Adapty sends Google the events that you select, such as started trials. Google uses these events to bid for people who are likely to start a trial or buy a subscription.

To select the events, in the **Events mapping** section of your configuration:

1. Turn on each event that Adapty should send to Google.
2. Give each event a name for Google Ads — select from a pre-defined list, or type in your own. The default selection depends on the [campaign type](ua-google-ads#supported-campaign-types):

   | Campaign type | Default name selection | Behavior with custom names |
   |---|---|---|
   | App campaigns for installs (UAC) | Google's standard app events, such as `in_app_purchase` | Adapty adds the event to Google as a custom app event. You don't need to set it up in advance |
   | All other campaign types | Adapty events, such as `trial_started` | Adapty adds a conversion action with the same name to your advertising account. You don't need to set it up in advance |

3. Click **Save**. The events you saved will be available when you create a campaign in Google Ads.

<ZoomImage id="ua-google-ads-events.webp" width="700px" alt="Events mapping section with the Install event turned on and mapped to first_open" />

#### Resolve naming conflicts

Your advertising account may already have a conversion action with the same name. In that case, what happens when you save the configuration depends on how that action was originally created:

- **By Adapty, or as an import of conversions from clicks**: Adapty reuses this conversion action. In its settings in Google Ads, make sure that **Action optimization** is set to primary and [**Count** is set to **Every**](google-ads-search-pmax-campaigns#set-the-conversion-actions-to-count-every-conversion).
- **In another way, such as with a website tag**: Adapty cannot reuse this name. It shows an error and doesn't save the configuration. Select a different name instead.

## Optional settings for trial events

:::warning
If an MMP, such as AppsFlyer or Adjust, also forwards `trial_started` or [`trial_active`](events#synthetic-events) to Google, Google counts the same trial more than once. Send trial events to Google from Adapty Attribution or the MMP, not both.
:::

### Revenue override

By default, Adapty sends the **Trial started** event to Google Ads without a revenue value, because no payment happens when a trial starts. With a revenue override, Adapty sends a value that you set: a percentage of the subscription price. For example, at 30%, Adapty sends 30% of the subscription price. **Trial converted** and **Subscription started** always have their actual revenue.

A revenue override changes the value that Google Ads receives for a trial. A [qualified trial](#qualified-trial) changes when Google Ads receives it. You can use both for the same event.

To report revenue to Google Ads for started trials:

1. In the **Events mapping** section, turn on the **Trial started** event. The **Revenue override** section appears.
2. In the **Revenue override** section, click **Add event**.
3. Select the name that you mapped to **Trial started**, such as `in_app_purchase`.
4. Enter the percentage of the subscription price to send, from 0 to 100.
5. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="revenue-override.webp" width="500px" alt="Trial started event mapped to in_app_purchase, and the Revenue override section set to 10% for in_app_purchase" />

### Qualified trial

:::important
Qualified trial reports require [App Store Server Notifications](enable-app-store-server-notifications) for iOS and [Real-time Developer Notifications](enable-real-time-developer-notifications-rtdn) for Android. The App Store and Google Play report trial cancellations to Adapty through these channels.
:::

By default, Adapty sends each started trial to Google immediately, even if the user cancels it soon after. With a qualified trial, Google only counts the trials that users keep — if the autorenewal isn't disabled a set number of hours after the trial start. Adapty sends the event with the original trial start time, so Google reports it on the day the trial started. If the event also has a revenue override, Adapty sends it with the overridden revenue.

To only send Google the trials that users keep:

1. In the **Events mapping** section, turn on the **Trial started** event.
2. In the **Qualified trial** section, click **Add event**.
3. Select the name that you mapped to **Trial started**, such as `in_app_purchase`.
4. Enter the number of hours to wait, from 1 to 24. Adapty sends the event about 45 minutes after the wait ends.
5. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="qualified-trial-google-ads.webp" width="700px" alt="Qualified trial section with the in_app_purchase target event held for one hour" />

If the user turns auto-renewal back on before the wait ends, Adapty sends the event as planned. Qualified trials only apply to trials from the App Store and Google Play.

#### Qualified trials vs. the Trial still active event

Adapty has two ways to only report the trials that users keep. For Google Ads, use a qualified trial: Adapty Attribution can't send the [Trial still active](events#synthetic-events) event to Google Ads. If another integration, such as AppsFlyer, also sends trials to Google Ads, Google Ads receives the same trial more than once.

| | Qualified trial | **Trial still active** event |
|---|---|---|
| Where you set it up | In Adapty Attribution, in a Meta, TikTok, or Google Ads configuration | In Adapty, in integrations such as AppsFlyer, Adjust, Firebase, or the webhook |
| What it does | Delays the **Trial started** event | Adds a separate event after **Trial started** |
| Wait time | 1 to 24 hours | 1 hour to 90 days |
| If the user turns off auto-renewal before the wait ends | Adapty doesn't send the event | Adapty doesn't send the event |
| Sent to Google Ads by Adapty Attribution | Yes | No |

## Next steps

After you connect your Google account, create a configuration appropriate for your campaign type.

- For an Android app: [Android App campaigns](google-ads-app-campaigns).
- For an iOS app or a web funnel: [Search and Performance Max campaigns](google-ads-search-pmax-campaigns).

If you later turn off an event or delete a configuration, its conversion actions stay in Google Ads: Adapty never edits or removes them. To clean up, remove the conversion actions that you no longer need in Google Ads.
