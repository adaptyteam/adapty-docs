## Before you start

Make sure these are in place before you start:

- **DNS access**: You can add records to your root domain.
- **Payments**: You have a Stripe account with your subscription products configured. To use a different provider, host the checkout yourself — see [Set up checkout](mail-checkout).

## 1. Choose your account type

:::warning
Choose carefully — a project you create yourself in Adapty Mail can't be linked to Adapty later.
:::

The account type determines what your Adapty Mail project can do:

| | Adapty-linked (recommended) | Standalone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sign up** | Sign in with your [Adapty account](https://app.adapty.io/) and open **Mail**. Adapty creates a project linked to your app. The data exchange between Adapty Mail and the SDK is disabled until the final step of the setup. You don't have to move your purchases to Adapty: the easy-to-setup [observer mode](observer-vs-full-mode), which only reports your transactions, is enough. | [Sign up for Adapty Mail only](https://mail.adapty.io/signup) with an email address and a password. No Adapty account and nothing to install in your app — you bring the data yourself, through FunnelFox or the API. |
| **Checkout page** (Step 7) | **Generate with AI** — a hosted checkout page built around your brand, with Stripe payments wired in — or your own hosted paywall | Your own hosted paywall |
| **Segments** | Import from Adapty, or build in Mail | Build in Mail |
| **Brand** — what your emails are generated from: name, logo, colors, and tone (Step 3) | Built from your app's store listing before you arrive, if it has one | Built from a store URL you paste |
| **Purchase data** (Step 2) | One click in **Settings** > **Project**; FunnelFox and the API also work | Secret key pasted into the Adapty dashboard or FunnelFox, or the API |
| **Team** | Teammates join when they open Mail from your Adapty company; email invites too | Email invites |

## 2. Connect your data

Adapty Mail picks recipients, places each person in a flow, and attributes revenue to the email that drove the purchase. Three kinds of data drive all of it:

- **Their email address** (mandatory): The address that receives your emails, subject to [address verification](mail-suppression#address-verification-failed).
- **Their [customer user ID](how-profiles-work)** (optional, but recommended): The unique identifier for the user. Adapty stores it in the user's profile so the same person is recognized across devices. Without one, Adapty creates a new profile every time they reinstall your app or log out.
- **Their subscription events feed** (optional, but recommended): Trials, renewals, cancellations, and refunds, as they happen. These allow you to target users based on their behavior, and tie revenue back to the email that drove the purchase. Without the event feed, the only sequence a user can receive is the one for people who never purchased.

Follow the instructions to create an **[entry point](mail-entry-points)** for this data:

- **Adapty**: Use this if your app already runs the Adapty SDK. Adapty forwards profiles, emails, and subscription events for you, so you need no server of your own. In your app, add user identification and email collection. See [Connect Adapty to Adapty Mail](mail-collect-emails).
- **Adapty Mail API**: Use this if you already run a backend that holds this data, if your app has no Adapty SDK, or if you import subscribers from somewhere else. You post profiles and transaction events yourself. The secret key on **Settings** > **Project** authenticates the requests. See [Send emails and transactions via the Adapty Mail API](mail-send-data-via-api).
- **FunnelFox**: Use this if you acquire and charge users through FunnelFox web funnels. FunnelFox already holds their emails, purchases, and subscription events, and forwards them to Adapty Mail on its own — no SDK work, and no requests to write. See [the FunnelFox integration guide](https://funnelfox.com/docs/integrations/email-providers/adapty-mail).
- **A combination**: You can use more than one entry point at once. Adapty Mail matches people by email address. Two sources reporting the same address land on one profile; two different addresses create separate profiles, even when `customer_user_id` is identical.

Adapty Mail starts sending emails once the rest of the setup is in place.

## 3. Set up your brand

Adapty Mail can't generate emails or web paywalls without a **brand**. Your brand stores the information Adapty Mail needs to match your app's look and voice — name, logo, colors, fonts, tone of voice, features, and reviews. You supply the store listing, and Adapty Mail builds the brand from it.

1. In Adapty Mail, open **Brand**.
2. If the page shows a filled-in brand, review it and move on. If it shows the **Set up your brand** screen, continue below.
3. Under **Store**, pick **App Store** or **Google Play**, then paste your listing URL.
   :::note
   The setup screen takes an App Store or Google Play listing and nothing else. If your app has no store listing — a web-to-app funnel, for example — contact Adapty support before you continue.
   :::
4. Click **Build my brand**. Adapty Mail fetches the page, parses reviews, and infers your brand voice. It usually finishes in under a minute.
5. To review sections, edit them by hand, or add landing-page and social sources, see [Brand](mail-brand).


## 4. Set up your sending domain

Adapty Mail sends emails from your private domain. You add DNS records once, and every campaign uses the same verified domain.

To register your first domain, in Adapty Mail go to **Settings** > **DNS** > the **Email domains** section.

1. In the **Domain** field, type your root domain. It must have a top-level domain suffix (`yourapp.com`). Adapty Mail does not accept subdomains (`app.yourapp.com`) or domains with second-level suffixes (`yourapp.co.uk`, `yourapp.com.br`).
2. In the **Sending subdomain** field, type the prefix your emails go out from, or leave it empty to use `mail`. Emails then come from `hello@mail.yourapp.com`.
3. Click **Add domain**.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-email-domains.webp" width="500px" />

4. In your domain registrar, add every DNS record the new domain card lists. They come in three groups: your sending subdomain's DKIM and MAIL FROM records, the records for the mailbox that warms the domain up, and the records for `go.yourapp.com`, which serves images and tracked links. For what each group authenticates, see [Which records you add](mail-sending-domain#2-add-the-dns-records). To take them all at once, click **Export**.
5. Optionally, add the suggested DMARC TXT record on your root domain (recommended).
6. Click **Check** to verify immediately, or leave the card and let Adapty Mail poll DNS.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-email-domains.webp" width="700px" />

Verification timing at a glance:

- **Automatic polling**: The first check runs 1 minute after you add the domain, then intervals double up to once per hour until the records are found.
- **Manual check**: Click **Check** any time to trigger an immediate check.
- **DNS propagation**: Usually minutes, up to 48 hours in rare cases.
- **Verification window**: 7 days. If it lapses, your DNS records stay in place — add your domain again in **Settings** > **DNS** to start a new window.

One verified domain is enough to start using Adapty Mail. A project can hold up to five domains, and you can add the rest later.

For details on each record type and domain warm-up, see [Set up your sending domain](mail-sending-domain).

## 5. Add your company address

Anti-spam laws require a physical postal address in every marketing email. Adapty Mail puts yours in the footer of every campaign email, and blocks campaign generation until you fill it in.

1. In Adapty Mail, go to **Settings** > **Company**.
2. In the **Company address** section, fill in **Legal company name**, **Address line 1**, **City**, **Postal code**, and **Country**. **Address line 2** and **State / Region** are optional.
3. Click **Save**.

## 6. Select the email destination type

Give your emails somewhere to land. An **email destination** is a page address you save and reuse — any number of campaigns can point at the same one. For different campaign types, Adapty Mail offers different destination types, covered in depth in [Email destinations](mail-destinations). A campaign without a destination cannot be saved. This guide builds a marketing campaign, which needs a **web paywall**.

| Destination type | Destination content | Where to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Web Paywall** | A checkout page in the browser, where purchases are web payments rather than App Store or Google Play transactions. | Marketing campaigns |
| **Deep Link** | One of the screens in your app. If the device doesn't have the app installed, the link lands at the store listing. | App activation emails, after a purchase on the web |
| **Cancellation Page** | Account and subscription management page — hosted by you or your billing provider. | One-off emails that tell a new subscriber where to manage their subscription |

In Adapty Mail, go to **Email Destinations**, keep **Web Paywall** selected, and click **Create**. Give the destination a **Name**.

<ZoomImage id="new-web-paywall.webp" width="500px" />

## 7. Set up a web paywall

Fill in the **URL** field on the destination you just created. Adapty Mail can build the paywall for you, or you can host your own:

| Option | How the page gets made | Requirements |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Generate with AI** | Adapty Mail builds it in the bundled builder, powered by [FunnelFox](https://funnelfox.com). | Stripe, and a project linked to an Adapty app |
| **Use your own hosted paywall** | You build and host it. Adapty Mail redirects to it with the recipient's details in the URL. | Any payment provider, plus your own code that carries the `external_profile_id` from the URL to a [transaction event](mail-send-data-via-api#send-transaction-events). |

### Option B: Use your own hosted paywall

1. Paste the URL of your hosted paywall into **URL**, with placeholders as query parameters. It must use HTTPS. Adapty Mail substitutes the placeholders per recipient so the checkout page can identify the visitor. Example:

   ```
   https://example.com/paywall?email={email}&adapty_profile_id={adapty.external_profile_id}
   ```

   The Create page lists the placeholders you can use, and blocks the save if it finds one it doesn't recognize. See [Set up checkout](mail-checkout#use-your-own-hosted-paywall) for what each one resolves to.
2. Click **Save**.

## 8. Generate a campaign with AI

A **campaign** is a sequence of up to 15 emails that one recipient receives over time. AI writes the whole sequence from the brand you set up in [Step 3](#3-set-up-your-brand): copy, design, hero images, and personalized checkout links.

1. In Adapty Mail, go to **Campaigns** and click **Create**.
2. In the **New marketing campaign** dialog, select why the customer is getting these emails: a purchase they never made, a renewal they cancelled, a payment that failed. You can't change this later, and the campaign only appears in the flow for the situation you pick. Click **Continue**.
3. Set the campaign name.
4. In the **Email Destination (Web Paywall)** dropdown, select the web paywall you added on the previous step.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-new-campaign.webp" width="500px" />

5. Click **Generate emails**.
6. Fill out the generation dialog — tone, language, an optional custom prompt (up to 2,000 characters), and the number of emails (1–15, default 4). See [Create a campaign](mail-create-campaign) for what each field does.

   <ZoomImage id="generate-emails.webp" width="500px" />

7. Click **Generate**. Generation usually takes a few minutes. The system times out after 5 minutes if it can't complete — retry if that happens.
8. Preview each email. The preview header has a **Theme toggle** (Auto, Light, Dark) that controls how the preview renders — generated content is identical across modes. You can regenerate individual emails, edit the copy, or open the HTML editor for fine-grained control.

   <ZoomImage id="generated-emails.webp" width="700px" />

9. Click **Create** to save the campaign.

Adapty Mail saves the campaign as a **draft**. It sends nothing until you attach it to a flow in the next step.

## 9. Launch a flow

Adapty Mail includes its own **flows**. A flow connects **triggers** (subscription events) with **campaigns** (email sequences). Each of the triggers — a subscription expiring, a payment failing, and so on — has its own flow. A flow can send one campaign to everyone who hits the trigger, or use **segments** to send different groups different campaigns.

1. In Adapty Mail, go to **Flows**, then open the trigger you want to configure:
   - **Never purchased** — users who signed up but haven't made a purchase yet.
   - **Renewal cancelled** — users who turned off auto-renewal but still have an active subscription.
   - **Billing issue** — payment failed, declined or expired card, or grace period.
   - **Expired** — subscription has lapsed and access is gone.
   - **Refunded** — users who requested a refund after purchase.

   See [Flows](mail-flows) for the goal and tone guidance behind each trigger.

2. Click **Create** to open the dialog.

   <ZoomImage id="create-flow-mail.webp" width="500px" />

3. In the dialog:
   - Pick a **Segment**. **All Users** targets everyone who hits this trigger, and it's enough to launch. To narrow the audience later, create a [different segment](mail-segments) — a filtered subsection of your users, based on their country, age, or subscription state.
   - Set content type to **Campaign** (the A/B Test option is covered in [A/B testing](mail-ab-testing)).
   - Select the **Campaign** you saved in [Step 8](#8-generate-a-campaign-with-ai).
4. Click **Save**. The flow is live — there's no launch button.
   - A user who hits the trigger and matches the segment joins the campaign immediately. The first email follows after its **Delay**.
   - Each email has its own **Delay** — how long after the previous one it goes out. You can edit them in the campaign editor.
   - Adapty Mail only sends between 08:00 and 21:00 in the recipient's timezone. A send due outside those hours shifts to the nearest edge of the window.
   - The first email is exempt when it's due within two hours of the trigger. It goes out on time, even at night.

:::note
You can add more rows to the same trigger, each with its own segment and campaign. Adapty Mail checks them top to bottom and uses the first segment that matches. Keep **All Users** last — Adapty Mail won't save a flow with it anywhere else.
:::

<ZoomImage id="mail-flow-details.webp" width="500px" />

:::tip
If new recipients must confirm their address before they receive marketing, turn on [double opt-in](mail-double-opt-in) before this step. Only profiles created after you turn it on are asked to confirm.
:::

## 10. Start sending

Your campaign is wired up but not firing yet — no user data is reaching Adapty Mail. Start the data flowing through the [entry point you chose](#2-connect-your-data). Whether your project is linked to Adapty (Step 1) changes only *how* you switch the Adapty feed on, not what arrives:

| Your data comes from | Your Mail project is linked to an Adapty app | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [The Adapty SDK](mail-collect-emails#5-enable-sending) | Yes | In Adapty Mail, go to **Settings** > **Project** and, in the **Data integration** section, click **Enable Adapty integration**. |
| [The Adapty SDK](mail-collect-emails#5-enable-sending) | No | Adapty can feed a project it isn't linked to — it only needs the key. Copy the secret key from **Settings** > **Project** and paste it in the Adapty dashboard under **Integrations** > **Adapty Mail**. |
| Your backend, via the [Adapty Mail API](mail-send-data-via-api) | Either | Start posting profiles and transaction events. Profiles you send before finishing this setup never receive anything, which is why this step comes last. |
| [FunnelFox](mail-funnelfox) | Either | Copy the secret key from **Settings** > **Project** and paste it in FunnelFox under **Integrations** > **Adapty Mail**. Data starts arriving within about a minute. |

From here, users who hit the trigger and match your segment enter the campaign. Before you let real users in, [test the whole chain](mail-testing) with one test user.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue                                       | Solution                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Generate emails** is greyed out           | Your brand or company address is missing. The button's tooltip names which. See [Step 3](#3-set-up-your-brand) and [Step 5](#5-add-your-company-address)     |
| Paywall generation is blocked               | The panel names the cause: no brand, no connected Stripe account, or no Stripe products with prices. Fix it, then **Refresh** the checklist              |
| Campaign won't save                         | Every campaign needs a destination. Create one in [Step 6](#6-select-the-email-destination-type)                                                                              |
| A destination can't be edited               | A destination becomes read-only once you attach it to a campaign. Create a new destination and select it instead.                     |
| DNS records won't verify                    | Check that they match exactly — no trailing dots, correct CNAME targets, one value per record. DNS can take up to 48 hours to propagate                          |
| Verification window expired                 | Add the domain again in **Settings** > **DNS** to start a new window. The records at your registrar still apply — nothing to re-add there                        |
| Email generation timed out                  | Generation stops after 5 minutes. Retry, and contact Adapty support if it keeps failing                                                                          |
| A test recipient never gets the email       | The profile's address may have failed the check Adapty Mail runs on every new profile. Test with a different address                                            |

For errors outside initial setup, see the troubleshooting sections in [Set up your sending domain](mail-sending-domain#troubleshooting) and [Set up checkout](mail-checkout#troubleshooting).
