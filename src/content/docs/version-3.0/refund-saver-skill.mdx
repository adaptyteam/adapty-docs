[Refund Saver](refund-saver) answers App Store refund requests and Google Play chargeback reviews for you, so the store can decline unfair ones. The refund-saver skill runs Refund Saver from your AI tool. Your agent reads which stores have the keys Refund Saver needs and turns Refund Saver on. It sets the refund preference of each store, the App Store consent policy, and the preference of a specific user. When Refund Saver doesn't turn on or answers no requests, the agent names the likely causes. The skill works through the [`adapty refund-saver` CLI commands](developer-cli-refund-saver) or the [Adapty MCP server](adapty-mcp).

:::note
Before the skill turns Refund Saver on or off, or changes a preference or a consent setting, it tells you in plain words what the change does. It runs the change only after your yes.
:::

## What you can ask

Ask the way you'd brief a colleague. You don't need to name the skill:

```
Turn on Refund Saver for my app.

Is Refund Saver on, and is anything missing?

Ask the App Store for a prorated refund instead of declining.

Grant every refund request from the user with profile ID <profile-id>.

Refund Saver won't turn on. Why?

Refund Saver is on, but it answers no requests.

Turn on Refund Saver for all my apps.
```

## Before you start

- **The Refund Saver add-on**: When the Refund Saver add-on isn't active for your account, Adapty refuses to turn Refund Saver on. Activate the add-on on the [**Subscription & Billing**](account#subscription--billing) tab of your account settings.
- **The store connection**: Each store you sell on must be connected to Adapty: the [App Store](app-store-connection-configuration) or [Google Play](google-play-store-connection-configuration).
- **Store notifications**: The store sends Adapty each refund request in a notification. Set up [App Store Server Notifications V2](enable-app-store-server-notifications) and [Google Play real-time developer notifications](enable-real-time-developer-notifications-rtdn).
- **Privacy policy and consent on the App Store**: Choose how your app gets user consent, with an opt-in prompt or opt-out terms, and disclose in your privacy policy that you share consumption data with Apple. See [Obtain user consent](refund-saver#obtain-user-consent).
- **An AI tool connected to your Adapty account**: The skill works through the [Adapty MCP server](adapty-mcp) or the [Adapty CLI](developer-cli). In Claude Code and Codex, the plugin adds the MCP server, so you only sign in. If neither is set up, the skill asks which one to use and sets it up.

## How the skill sets up Refund Saver

1. **Reads the state**: The skill reads whether Refund Saver is on for the app, and whether each store has the keys Refund Saver needs: the [In-App Purchase key](generate-in-app-purchase-key) for the App Store, and the service account key for Google Play. For a store without its key, the skill names what to add in **App settings** > **iOS SDK** or **Android SDK**.
2. **Names what it can't read**: No command shows the store notifications, your privacy policy, or how your app asks for consent. The skill asks you to verify that the store notifications reach Adapty, and links the [store prerequisites](refund-saver#store-specific-prerequisites) for the privacy policy and consent.
3. **Turns Refund Saver on**: When at least one store you sell on has the keys, the skill asks for your yes, then turns Refund Saver on for both stores. It shows you Adapty's note about the Refund Saver add-on. A store without the keys gets no answers until you add them. With no store ready, the skill turns nothing on and names what to add.
4. **Sets the preferences you choose**: For most apps, the skill recommends the default refund preference, **Decline first refund request, grant all next**. Each change to a store's preference, to the consent policy, or to one user's settings takes its own yes.

Refund Saver works when answered requests appear in the [Refund Saver](https://app.adapty.io/refund-saver) section, or when the skill lists them on your request. Adapty answers the first one when the next refund request comes in, which can take weeks in an app with few purchases.

The consent policy decides whether Refund Saver sends Apple the data of a user with no recorded consent. Match the policy to how your app asks for consent. The skill can't see your app's consent prompt, so it asks you which policy fits. To learn what each option does, see [Set the default consent policy](refund-saver#set-the-default-consent-policy).

To change one user's settings, give the skill the user's **Adapty profile ID**, not the customer user ID from your app. Find it on the user's [profile](profiles-crm) in the dashboard. The skill can also read it from a refund request that Refund Saver answered.

To turn Refund Saver on for several apps, ask for all of them at once. The skill reads each app, then shows one confirmation that names every app it changes.

## What the skill asks before each change

Only your answer to the skill's question counts. A general "go ahead" before the skill shows the change doesn't. If you say no, the skill changes nothing.

| Change | What the skill tells you |
|---|---|
| Turn on Refund Saver | From now on, Adapty answers this app's App Store refund requests and Google Play chargeback reviews, with each store's preference. The skill names the App Store consent policy. With **Opt-out**, Adapty also sends Apple the consumption data of users who have no recorded consent. Under either policy, your privacy policy must say that you share consumption data with Apple for refund requests. |
| Turn off Refund Saver | Adapty stops answering this app's App Store refund requests and Google Play chargeback reviews. The Refund Saver add-on stays active until you cancel it on the **Subscription & Billing** tab. |
| Change a store's preference or the consent policy | The current value and the new value. The new value applies to every new request from that store. |
| Change one user's settings | The current and the new preference or consent of that user. A grant or a decline for the user wins over the store's preference, on both stores. |

In a scheduled run or a script, with no one to answer, the skill only reads. For a change, it reports what the change does and the command for you to run.

## When Refund Saver doesn't work as expected

- **Adapty refuses to turn Refund Saver on**: The skill tells you the reason Adapty returns, and who fixes it:
  - The Refund Saver add-on isn't active for your account. Activate it on the **Subscription & Billing** tab.
  - The Apple credentials of your account don't work. Fix them in **App settings**.
  - Refund Saver can't be turned on for your account. Contact Adapty support.

  After a fix, ask the skill to try again. It doesn't retry on its own.
- **Adapty refuses every change**: Your Adapty account can view the app but has no edit rights in its company. Ask a company owner or admin for edit rights.
- **Refund Saver is on, but it answers no requests**: The skill reads the requests that Refund Saver answered and the keys of each store. Then it names the possible causes:
  - A store has no keys, or the store doesn't accept them. Verify them in **App settings**.
  - The store notifications don't reach Adapty.
  - The consent policy is **Opt-in**, and your app never sends Adapty a user's consent. Adapty then sends Apple no consumption data.
  - On Google Play, Refund Saver answers only chargebacks. Google decides ordinary refund requests without input from Adapty. See [How the stores differ](refund-saver#how-the-stores-differ).
  - An app with few purchases can go weeks without a refund request.
- **Google Play outcomes appear late**: Google doesn't notify Adapty when it declines a chargeback, so Adapty polls the order status until the review is settled. Google Play outcomes reach the requests list and the savings later than App Store ones.

## With other skills

- **See what Refund Saver saved**: The [growth-advisor skill](export-analytics-with-ai) reads the amount Refund Saver kept and its win rate, and compares both with your refunds. When your refunds are high and Refund Saver is off, it offers to turn Refund Saver on. When Refund Saver has saved money in an app, it reads your other apps and offers to turn Refund Saver on where it's off. On your yes, it hands the change to the refund-saver skill.
- **Integrate the Adapty SDK**: When the [adapty-integration skill](adapty-sdk-integration-skill) finishes, it offers to set up Refund Saver.

## Limits

- **No add-on changes**: The skill can't activate or cancel the Refund Saver add-on. Do this on the **Subscription & Billing** tab of your account settings.
- **No app code**: The skill doesn't write the app code that sends Adapty a user's preference or consent. To add this code, see [Manage Refund Saver from your app code](refund-saver#manage-refund-saver-from-your-app-code).
- **No estimate of saved refunds**: The store decides each request, so the skill doesn't predict how much Refund Saver will keep.

To run the commands yourself, without the skill, see [Refund Saver commands](developer-cli-refund-saver).
