Use this integration if you advertise an Android app, an iOS app, or a web funnel with Google Ads. When you connect Google Ads to Adapty Attribution:

- Google bids for people who are likely to start a trial or buy a subscription. Adapty sends these trials and purchases to Google.
- Adapty Attribution shows the revenue and ROAS of each campaign, with the spend that Adapty pulls from Google Ads.

## Before you begin

Make sure that:

- You have a Google Ads account in the correct mode:
  - ✅ **Expert Mode**: You can create every [campaign type that Adapty Attribution supports](#supported-campaign-types).
  - ❌ **Smart Mode**: Only works with Smart campaigns, which are incompatible with Adapty Attribution. A new account starts in Smart Mode if you create a Smart campaign when you sign up. Switch to Expert Mode from the **Tools** menu (this action is irreversible).
- Your Google Ads account has a payment method.
- The Adapty SDK in your app has [Adapty Attribution enabled](user-acquisition#step-1-enable-adapty-attribution-in-the-sdk).

## Set up the integration

1. In Adapty Attribution, [connect your Google account](ua-google-ads-settings#connect-your-google-account).
2. To create a configuration in Adapty Attribution and set up your campaigns in Google Ads, follow the guide for what you promote:
   - For an Android app: [Android App campaigns](google-ads-app-campaigns).
   - For an iOS app or a web funnel: [Search and Performance Max campaigns](google-ads-search-pmax-campaigns).

You create a separate configuration in each guide, and one app can have both configurations.

:::important
You don't need to set up SKAdNetwork (SKAN), the Apple framework that reports ad installs without identifying users. Adapty Attribution doesn't use it. If you use SKAN for other purposes, keep your SKAN settings: they don't affect Adapty Attribution.
:::

## Campaign types

### Supported campaign types

| Campaign type | Where the ads run | Tab in Adapty Attribution | Compatible app store listings | Compatible with web funnels |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| App campaigns for installs (UAC) | Google Search, Google Play, YouTube, and the Display Network | **App Campaigns** | Google Play | ❌ |
| Search | Google Search results, for the keywords you add | **Other Campaigns** | App Store, Google Play | ✅ |
| Performance Max | All Google channels from one campaign: Search, YouTube, Display, Discover, Gmail, and Maps | **Other Campaigns** | App Store, Google Play | ✅ |
| Demand Gen | YouTube in-feed and Shorts, Discover, and Gmail, as image and video ads | **Other Campaigns** | App Store, Google Play | ✅ |
| Display | Banner ads on sites and apps in the Google Display Network | **Other Campaigns** | App Store, Google Play | ✅ |
| Video | Video ads on YouTube | **Other Campaigns** | App Store, Google Play | ✅ |
| Shopping | Product ads from your Google Merchant Center feed | **Other Campaigns** | App Store, Google Play | ✅ |

### Incompatible campaign types

- **Re-engagement campaigns**: App campaigns of the "App engagement" subtype. They show ads to people who already have your app. Adapty only sends Google the events of new installs.
- **Pre-registration campaigns**: App campaigns of the "App pre-registration" subtype, for apps that aren't released on Google Play yet.
- **Smart campaigns**: The only campaign type that Google Ads accounts in Smart Mode can create.

## How it works

The attribution mechanism is different for different campaign types:

- **App campaigns for installs (UAC)**:
  - Adapty sends each new install to Google.
  - Google verifies whether the user clicked or otherwise interacted with an App campaign ad before the install.
  - If so, Google returns the campaign, ad group, and ad to Adapty.
- **All other campaign types**:
  - Google Ads sends each ad click to Adapty.
  - Adapty matches installs and web purchases to these clicks.

If the user clicked ads from several campaigns before the install, Adapty credits the install to the [latest click](adapty-user-acquisition#channels).

### App campaigns (Android)

Adapty connects to Google Ads as a third-party app analytics provider. You link Adapty Attribution to your app in Google Ads with an App Link ID. These campaigns do not require a tracking template.

1. A user installs your app from an App campaign ad and opens it for the first time.
2. Adapty sends the install to Google through the link ID.
3. Google matches the install to the App campaign ad and returns the campaign, ad group, and ad to Adapty.
4. Adapty sends the user's trials and purchases to Google through the same link ID.

### Other campaigns (iOS and Android)

You add the tracking template from Adapty Attribution to the campaign in Google Ads. A tracking template is a link that Google Ads loads in the background every time someone clicks your ad.

1. A user clicks the ad, and Google adds a click ID to the click. For iOS users who don't allow tracking, Google uses a privacy-safe click ID, `gbraid` or `wbraid`, instead of `gclid`.
2. Google Ads sends the user to the store or your web funnel. At the same time, Google Ads loads the tracking template in the background. Through it, Google Ads passes the click ID and the campaign details to Adapty.
3. Adapty matches the install or web purchase to that click:
   - **iOS installs**: Adapty uses a probabilistic match to select the click that most likely led to the install.
   - **Android installs**: Adapty uses a match by click ID. If Google Play passes no click ID to your app, Adapty uses a probabilistic match.
   - **Web purchases**: Adapty reads the click details that your web funnel passes at checkout.
4. Adapty sends conversions to the Google Ads account you select, matched by the click ID.

#### How Adapty matches installs and purchases to campaigns

Adapty matches each in-app purchase to the campaign of its install. To match an install to a click, Adapty uses one of two methods:

| Method | How Adapty credits the install | Time limit between the click and the install |
|---|---|---|
| Match by click ID | To the click with the ID that Google Play passes to your app | 168 hours (7 days) by default, up to 720 hours |
| Probabilistic match | To the most likely recent click from the same IP address range and operating system | 6 hours by default, up to 24 hours |

For a probabilistic match, Adapty selects the click that scores best on time since the click, device language, and country. Adapty doesn't use the advertising ID (IDFA), so probabilistic matching covers iOS users who don't allow tracking.

Adapty can't match an install probabilistically if the device switches networks between the click and the install, for example from Wi-Fi to cellular. On networks that many devices share, such as mobile carrier networks, Adapty matches fewer installs probabilistically. Adapty Attribution shows an unmatched install without campaign details: under the **Google Play** channel if it has a click ID, and under **Organic** if it doesn't.

#### Change the time limits for matching

The time limits don't apply to App campaigns, because Google decides which installs came from them.

1. In the header of Adapty Attribution, select the app to advertise.
2. Go to **Integrations** > **Google Ads**. On the **Other Campaigns** tab, open the configuration that you copied the tracking template from.
3. Select the **Settings** tab.
4. In the **New user attribution** section, enter each limit in hours:
   - **Deterministic Matching Window**: For a match by click ID.
   - **Probabilistic Matching Window**: For a probabilistic match. If you set more than 6 hours, clicks more than 6 hours before the install must also match the device model and OS version of the install.

   Keep the switches of both fields turned on: without probabilistic matching, Adapty can't match iOS installs.

## Keep Google Ads data accurate in Adapty Attribution

To break down Google Ads data in [Adapty Attribution analytics](ua-analytics), select one of these options in **Group by**:

| Group by | What it is in Google Ads | Spend reported for | Country breakdown |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Campaign** | A set of ad groups that share one budget, bidding strategy, and targeting | All campaign types | Exact, as Google reports it |
| **Adset** | An ad group: a group of ads in a campaign. In a Search campaign, an ad group also contains keywords. Performance Max campaigns use asset groups instead | All campaign types except Performance Max | Exact, as Google reports it |
| **Ad** | The text, image, or video that users see | All campaign types except Performance Max | Estimated: Google doesn't report ads by country, so Adapty splits the numbers of each ad across countries the same way the numbers of its ad group split |
| **Keyword** | A word or phrase that you add to an ad group of a Search campaign. Google shows the ads of the ad group in searches that match the keyword | Search campaigns only | Not available |

To keep the data accurate and read it correctly:

- **Promote one app per campaign**: Adapty adds the full spend of a campaign to each app that the campaign promotes. For a campaign that leads to a web funnel, that's the app that you copied the tracking template from.
- **Paste the tracking template into all your campaigns**: It's required for every non-UAC campaign. If you omit it, Adapty Attribution shows the installs, but not the campaign details: Android installs under the **Google Play** channel, and iOS installs under **Organic**. Adapty Attribution can still show the spend of the campaign, but not its revenue, so its ROAS looks low.
- **Remove other tracking templates from your campaigns**: Google Ads doesn't send Adapty the clicks on an ad group, ad, or keyword with [its own tracking template](google-ads-search-pmax-campaigns#add-the-tracking-template-to-an-existing-campaign).
- **Expect recent spend to change**: Google keeps adjusting spend, impressions, and clicks for recent days. Every 4 hours, Adapty refreshes these numbers for the last 3 days, including today.
- **Expect daily spend and revenue to use different time zones**: Adapty Attribution shows Google Ads spend by day in the time zone of your Google Ads account. It shows installs and revenue by day in UTC. If the two time zones differ, daily spend and daily revenue cover different hours. To show installs and revenue in another time zone, contact [Adapty support](mailto:support@adapty.io).
- **Use Adapty Attribution for revenue and ROAS**: The conversion values in Google Ads don't match your actual revenue:
  - Adapty doesn't send refunds to Google.
  - In Other campaigns, Google only counts the events that happen within 30 days after the ad click.
  - In App campaigns, Adapty only sends Google the events that happened in the last 90 days. Adapty sends most events soon after they happen, so the limit doesn't affect renewals long after the install.
