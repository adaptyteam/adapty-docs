You can create user [segments](segments) directly from [Adapty Ads Manager](adapty-ads-manager) by picking the campaigns, ad groups, or keywords users came from. It makes it easy to personalize paywalls and offers based on an acquisition source, without setting up segment conditions manually.

After creating a segment, you can use it to assign different products and prices, run A/B tests, and customize paywall appearance.

:::tip
To create a segment for each campaign from a script or an AI coding tool, use [`adapty asa segments create`](developer-cli-ads-manager-reference#segments) in the Adapty CLI.
:::

## Use cases

Here are some examples of how segments created from Apple Ads data can be used in practice:

- **Keyword-based paywalls**. Show a feature-focused paywall to users from high-intent keywords, and a general paywall to users from broader discovery keywords.

- **Campaign-level offers**. Offer longer trials or special pricing to users from selected Apple Ads campaigns, while keeping a standard offer for other users.

- **Creative-to-paywall consistency**. Route users from ad groups promoting specific features to paywalls that highlight those features first.

- **High-ROI campaign optimization**. Show a premium-first, full-price paywall to users from campaigns that consistently drive higher lifetime value.

## Open the Segments page

The **Segments** page lists the segments you built from Apple Ads for one app, the ads each segment includes, its profile count, and the placements that use it.

To open the page, in the Adapty Ads Manager sidebar, click **Segments**, and then choose an app.

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-page.webp" width="700px" alt="Segments page with three Apple Ads segments" />

Adapty creates the segments in the Adapty app that matches your Apple Ads app by **Apple app ID** or **Bundle ID**. If no Adapty app matches, the page asks you to choose one in the **Choose Adapty app** dropdown. Your browser remembers this choice, but other browsers and teammates don't see it. To match the apps for everyone, add the **Apple app ID** to the Adapty app's [App Store integration settings](app-store-connection-configuration#step-1-provide-bundle-id-and-apple-app-id).

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-link-app.webp" width="700px" alt="Choosing the Adapty app for an Apple Ads app" />

## Create a segment

A segment includes users who installed your app after tapping an ad from the campaigns, ad groups, or keywords you add to it.

To create a segment:

1. Open the segment editor in one of these ways:
    - On the **Segments** page, click **+ New segment**.
    - In **Ads Manager**, switch to the **Campaigns**, **Ad groups**, or **Keywords** tab and select the rows to include. Then click **Actions > Create segment from campaigns/ad groups/keywords**. All selected rows must belong to one app.

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-actions-menu.webp" width="500px" alt="Segment items in the Ads Manager Actions menu" />

2. In **Name**, enter the segment name. Adapty adds the fixed `[ASA]` prefix to the name, so you can tell Apple Ads segments from other segments in Adapty.
3. To add ads to the segment, under **Who's in it**, click **+ Add campaigns, ad groups or keywords**. Select the campaigns, ad groups, or keywords and click **Add**. A segment can include keywords, or campaigns and ad groups, but not both.

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-picker.webp" width="500px" alt="Picking campaigns, ad groups, or keywords for a segment" />

4. Click **Create segment**.

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-new.webp" width="500px" alt="New segment with one ad group" />

If you add both campaigns and ad groups, Adapty saves the campaigns as the ad groups they contain when you save the segment. Ad groups you add to these campaigns later don't join the segment.

:::tip
The **Ideas from your ads** chips on the **Segments** page suggest segments built from your ads, such as one ad group name used in several campaigns. Click an idea to open it in the editor, then change anything before you save.
:::

## Use the segment in a placement

Adapty doesn't add a new segment to any placement. After you create a segment, the **Segment created** dialog lists the placements of the Adapty app.

To show users in the segment their own paywall, onboarding, or A/B test, open a placement and [add the segment as an audience](add-audience-paywall-ab-test).

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-created.webp" width="500px" alt="Segment created dialog with placements" />

To add the segment to a placement later, on the **Segments** page, click **Use** in the segment's **Used in** column.

## Add ads to an existing segment

To add campaigns, ad groups, or keywords to a segment you already have:

1. In **Ads Manager**, switch to the **Campaigns**, **Ad groups**, or **Keywords** tab and select the rows.
2. Click **Actions > Add to segment…**.
3. Select the segment. A segment of keywords can't take campaigns or ad groups, and a segment of campaigns and ad groups can't take keywords, so the dialog disables segments of the other kind.
4. In the segment editor, review the ads and click **Save**. Adapty doesn't change the segment until you save it.

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-add-to.webp" width="500px" alt="Choosing a segment to add ad groups to" />

## Edit or delete a segment

To open a segment in the editor, on the **Segments** page, click the segment's row. There, you can rename the segment and add or remove ads.

If a placement uses the segment as an audience, saving the segment immediately changes which users see that audience's paywall or onboarding. The **Used in** section of the editor lists these placements.

<ZoomImage id="asa-segments-edit.webp" width="500px" alt="Editing a segment that is used in two placements" />

To delete a segment, in the editor, click **Delete** and confirm. Adapty deletes the segment and removes it from the audiences of every placement that uses it.

## Segments that don't appear on the page

The **Segments** page shows only segments with the `[ASA]` prefix whose only condition is a list of Apple Ads campaigns, ad groups, or keywords. If you edit an `[ASA]` segment in **Profiles & Segments** and add another condition, the **Segments** page hides it. To find the segment, click **open in Adapty** under the segment list. It opens **Profiles & Segments**.
