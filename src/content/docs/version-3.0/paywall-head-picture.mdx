Give any screen a background in the **Fill** section of [**Screen settings**](paywall-layout-and-products#screen-settings):

- **Single-layer background**: A color, gradient, image, or video.
- **Multi-layer background**: For example, an image with a [tint](#tint-a-background).

If you [add a dark theme](paywall-dark-mode#add-a-dark-theme) and apply a [reusable style](builder-reusable-styles#color-styles), you can [set different backgrounds](#show-a-different-background-in-dark-mode) for the light theme and the dark theme.

:::tip
To turn a background into a hero image, [put your content in a sliding sheet](#use-a-background-as-a-hero-image).
:::

## Image

<ZoomImage id="single-layer-image-fill.webp" width="300px" alt="Screen settings with one Image layer in the Fill section, and the image picker on the Custom tab" float="right"/>

Upload a `.JPG`, `.PNG`, or `.WEBP` file up to 20 MB. The image scales to cover the entire background.

:::note
Background images and videos fill the entire viewport, including areas behind the notch and system bars — even when **Safe area** is enabled. Keep important content away from the edges to avoid clipping.
:::

To swap the background image at runtime from your app code, enable a [custom media ID](custom-media#custom-media-id).

## Video

<ZoomImage id="single-layer-video-fill.webp" width="300px" alt="Screen settings with one Video layer in the Fill section, and the video picker with the Loop checkbox" float="right"/>

Upload an `.MP4` or `.WEBM` file up to 50 MB. The preview shows a still frame — but the video plays on device at runtime.

Toggle **Loop** to replay the video continuously.

To swap the background video at runtime, enable a [custom media ID](custom-media#custom-media-id).

## Solid color

<ZoomImage id="single-layer-color-fill.webp" width="300px" alt="Screen settings with one solid color layer in the Fill section, and the color picker on the Custom tab" float="right"/>

Enter a six-digit hex value, such as `#FFFFFF`, and set opacity from 0 to 100%. A [shorter value blocks publishing](flow-common-issues#publishing-fails-after-entering-a-shorthand-hex-color).

## Gradient

<ZoomImage id="gradient-picker.webp" width="300px" alt="The gradient picker with three stops, the Direction slider, and the Stops list" float="right"/>

Build a multi-stop linear gradient:

- **Direction** — rotate the gradient from 0 to 360°.
- **Stops** — to reposition a stop, drag it along the bar. To add a stop, click the bar or <Inline id="plus.svg" alt="Add gradient stop" /> next to **Stops**. To change the position, six-digit hex value, or opacity of a stop, edit its row.
- **Opacity** — the slider on the layer's row scales every stop at once, so the fade between them stays intact.

## Use a background as a hero image

:::link
Main article: [Sliding sheet](builder-containers#sliding-sheet)
:::

In flows, you build a hero image from a screen background and a **Sliding sheet**. The sheet covers the lower part of the screen and rises over the background as the user scrolls.

<DocVideo id="sliding-sheet-demo.mp4" width="300px" alt="The finished screen: the sheet starts partway up over the hero photo, rises to cover it as the screen scrolls, then settles back to its start position" />

1. Set the screen's **Fill** to **Image** or **Video**, and upload the hero image.

    <ZoomImage id="hero-1-background-fill.webp" width="700px" alt="Screen settings with Fill set to Image, and the hero photo filling the phone preview" />

2. Open the **Add element** panel and select **Layout** > **Sliding sheet**.
3. Set the sheet's **Start position** — the share of the screen the sheet covers, measured from the bottom. At the default 55%, the background shows through the top 45%.

    <ZoomImage id="hero-3-start-position.webp" width="700px" alt="Sliding sheet settings with Start position highlighted and Height locked to Auto, beside the phone preview showing the selected sheet over the hero photo" />

4. Add your content inside the sheet.
5. To overlay a headline or logo over the hero image, add those elements outside the sheet. They render over the screen background.
6. Change the **Start position** to resize the sheet, if necessary. You cannot set an absolute height for this element.

## Tint a background

Copy set over an image or a video is legible only where the media behind it happens to be dark enough. To even out that surface, stack a semi-transparent layer over the media:

1. In **Screen settings**, set the **Fill** layer to your image or video.
2. To add a second layer, click <Inline id="plus.svg" alt="Plus" /> next to **Fill**. The new layer appears at the top of the **Fill** section and covers the media.
3. Click the layer's swatch and select **Gradient** <Inline id="fill-gradient.svg" alt="Gradient" />. To darken only the edge that carries your copy, run the gradient from an opaque stop at that edge to a transparent stop at the other.
4. To set the strength of the tint, drag the slider on the layer's row.

To change the order of the layers, drag their rows. To remove a layer, click <Inline id="close.svg" alt="Close" /> in its row.

<ZoomImage id="flow-builder-bg-tint.webp" width="700px" alt="A gradient tint layer at 67% over an image layer in the Fill list" />

## Show a different background in dark mode

:::link
Main article: [Dark mode](paywall-dark-mode)
:::

To show a different background in dark mode:

1. If the flow has no dark theme, [add it](paywall-dark-mode#add-a-dark-theme).
2. [Save the background layer as a color style](builder-reusable-styles#create-a-color-style).
3. If the background has a [tint](#tint-a-background), save the tint layer as a color style too.
4. [Change the dark value](paywall-dark-mode#configure-the-dark-theme) of each style.
