AI Growth Advisor tells you what to change in your app to make more money, and helps you prove it with an A/B test. Your AI agent does the analysis and the setup with the [growth-advisor skill](export-analytics-with-ai). The **AI Growth Advisor** page in the Adapty Dashboard keeps the result: every growth hypothesis your team decided to work on, from idea to A/B test result.

<ZoomImage id="ai-growth-advisor-hypotheses.webp" width="700px" alt="The AI Growth Advisor page with two hypotheses on the Hypotheses tab, each with its placement, flow or segment, rationale, and expected impact" />

## How it works

1. **The agent reads your numbers.** Revenue, conversions at each step from install to paid, renewals, refunds, and your top countries, along with your placements, flows, products, prices, and past A/B tests. It reads everything in your account and changes nothing without your yes.
2. **It grades them against the market.** Each part of your business is called strong, typical, or weak against market medians for your region and category, from Adapty's State of Paywalls and State of In-App Subscriptions reports. Weak parts are ranked by the dollars a month they cost you.
3. **It suggests hypotheses.** Each hypothesis is one change with the reason your app needs it, the expected result from real subscription A/B tests, and how long the A/B test takes at your traffic.
4. **You keep the ones worth testing.** The hypotheses you add land on this page, where the whole team sees them. The ones you skip are archived with your reason, so the agent doesn't suggest them again.
5. **The agent sets up the A/B test.** It builds the flow copy, product, or segment the test needs, creates the test, and links it to its hypothesis. Each change waits for your confirmation.
6. **You decide the result.** When the test has enough purchases, the agent shows each variant's numbers and asks you which one won. The result is saved on the hypothesis, and the next hypothesis can build on it.

The agent starts every conversation from this list, so it doesn't suggest what you already tested, and a new teammate sees the whole history of what was tested and why.

## Before you start

- **An AI agent with the Adapty plugin**: The growth-advisor skill is part of the [Adapty plugin](manage-adapty-with-ai). It works in coding agents such as Claude Code and Codex, and in claude.ai.
- **Data in Adapty**: The skill grades your app against the market from your own revenue, conversions, and renewals. Without data, it suggests a starting plan from market medians.

## Get your first hypotheses

1. To install the Adapty plugin, follow [Install the skill](export-analytics-with-ai#install-the-skill).

2. To get the prompt for your app, on the **AI Growth Advisor** page, click **Copy to agent**. If your list already has hypotheses, click **How it works** and copy the prompt there. The prompt asks the agent to audit your app and build a test plan, and names your app ID, so the agent reads the right app.

3. Paste the prompt into your agent. The agent audits your app and replies with a list of hypotheses, then asks which to add to your list and which to skip.

4. Tell the agent which hypotheses to add. The agent saves them, and they show on the **AI Growth Advisor** page.

<ZoomImage id="ai-growth-advisor-empty.webp" width="500px" alt="The empty AI Growth Advisor page with two steps: install the Adapty Skills plugin, then copy the prompt to your agent" />

You don't need to start from the dashboard. Whenever the growth-advisor skill suggests hypotheses, for example after you ask "What should I test next?", it asks which to add.

:::tip
A hypothesis you skip is saved to **Archived** with the reason you give, so the agent doesn't suggest it again. Tell the agent "not now" to keep it among your hypotheses instead.
:::

## Hypotheses by stage

The tabs follow a hypothesis from idea to result:

| Tab | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Hypotheses** | Hypotheses waiting for an A/B test, including those whose A/B test is drafted and ready to launch |
| **Testing** | Hypotheses with a running A/B test |
| **Completed** | Hypotheses with a finished A/B test, and changes put live without a test |
| **Archived** | Hypotheses you archived, and those you skipped when the agent suggested them |

When a tab shows more than one type of hypothesis, you can filter it by type: **Visual**, **Pricing**, **Geo-pricing**, **Audience**, or **Other**. The tab and the filter are part of the page URL, so you can share a view with your team.

Each hypothesis card shows:

- **What changes and why**: The title, what changes, and under **Rationale**, why your app needs the change.
- **Where it runs**: The **Placement**, and the **Flow** or **Segment** it changes. A placement, flow, or segment deleted in Adapty is marked **Deleted**.
- **Expected impact**: The metric that judges the A/B test, such as ARPU or CR to trial, and the expected range. The range is an estimate from market data and real A/B tests. Your result may differ.
- **Based on**: The hypothesis this one grew from, such as the next step after a finished A/B test.

## Continue a hypothesis with your agent

Each card has a button that copies a prompt for your agent. The prompt names the hypothesis, so the agent reads its current state in Adapty and continues from there. You don't need to explain where you left off.

What the card offers depends on its status:

| Status | Buttons | What the agent does with the prompt |
|---|---|---|
| No status | **Copy to agent** | Builds what the A/B test needs, such as a copy of the flow or a new product, and sets up the test |
| **Ready to launch** | **Launch test**, **Copy to agent** | Reviews the draft A/B test with you and helps you launch it. **Launch test** opens the draft, where you start the test |
| **Test running** | **View test**, **Copy to agent** | Reports how the test is doing and whether it's ready to stop |
| **Needs review** | **View results**, **Discuss with agent** | Reviews the result with you and asks whether the test was won, lost, or inconclusive. The card may show the result Adapty suggests |
| **Won**, **Lost**, **Inconclusive** | **View results**, **Discuss with agent** | Reviews the result and suggests the next step |
| **Applied without test** | **Discuss with agent** | Reviews how the change is doing since it went live and suggests the next step |

<ZoomImage id="ai-growth-advisor-completed.webp" width="700px" alt="The Completed tab with three hypotheses: one marked Won, one marked Needs review, and one Applied without test" />

A test stopped with a winner moves its hypothesis to **Completed** by itself. A test that ended with no winner shows **Needs review** until you tell the agent the result.

:::tip
To hear when a test is ready to decide without asking, have the agent check it on a schedule. See [Schedule recurring checks](growth-advisor-recurring-checks).
:::

## Archive a hypothesis

To remove a hypothesis from your list, on its card, click **Archive**. Archiving changes only the list: the hypothesis's products, A/B tests, flows, and placements keep working in Adapty. To bring it back, click **Undo** in the message that opens, or later, on the **Archived** tab, click **Restore**.

A restored hypothesis comes back as a new hypothesis, without the A/B test it had.

## Hypotheses from Autopilot v1

AI Growth Advisor used to build a growth plan from a paywall and market analysis in the dashboard. That analysis is no longer available. Hypotheses from those growth plans stay in your list with the **Autopilot v1** badge. **Copy to agent** on such a card also copies its title, change, and rationale, so the agent can pick it up.
