Adapty Attribution’s TikTok for Business integration lets you track and optimize campaign performance in TikTok.

:::tip
See our [guide on setting up ads in TikTok for Business](tiktok-create-campaign).
:::

## Step 1. Connect your TikTok account

1. Go to **Integrations > TikTok Ads** from the left sidebar and click **Continue with TikTok**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-connect-tiktok.webp" width="700px" />

2. Sign in using your TikTok account and click **Continue**.

3. Review the requested permissions and click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-tiktok-sign.webp" width="500px" />

After that, all your ad accounts will be added to Adapty Attribution. You can proceed with adding campaigns.

## Step 2. Add campaigns

A single campaign can cover the App Store, Google Play, and your web funnel at once. Two pieces make the connection work:

- **Tracking link**: Goes into your ad. When a user taps the ad, Adapty records the click, sends the user on to the destination, and later attributes the install and purchases to that ad. Each destination gets its own link.
- **Pixel**: Selected in the campaign, from the pixels in your TikTok account. Carries those conversions back to TikTok, so TikTok can optimize the campaign on real purchases. One pixel can serve all three destinations, or each destination can have its own.

To add a TikTok for Business campaign to Adapty Attribution and track how your TikTok ads work in Adapty:

1. Switch to the **Web Campaigns** tab and click **Create configuration**.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-new-campaign-tiktok.webp" width="500px" />

2. In the **General** tab, expand the **iOS** and/or **Android** section and paste App Store and/or Google Play application URLs.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-url.webp" width="900px" />

3. Copy the **Click link** field value. Then, in TikTok Ads Manager, when creating your ad, paste this value in the **Tracking URL** field under the **Advanced Settings** section. This will allow Adapty to connect installs and purchases to ads in TikTok.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-lnk.webp" width="900px" />

4. (Optional) To send the conversion events back to TikTok, you can also associate your pixels from TikTok with campaigns in Adapty Attribution. To do that, select one of your existing pixels in the **Pixel** dropdown. After selecting a pixel, you can click **Send test event** to verify the connection.

   The **Web funnel** section works the same way. Purchases on a web funnel happen outside your app, so TikTok learns about them only through the pixel you select here. The same section gives you the snippet to [install the pixel on your funnel](ua-web-pixel) and the parameters to [tag your ad URLs](ua-tag-ad-urls).

A different pixel per destination keeps iOS, Android, and web purchases separate for optimization and reporting; the same pixel everywhere combines them. The **Web Campaigns** list shows that choice per configuration, under **Pixel (iOS)**, **Pixel (Android)**, and **Pixel (Web)**, as the pixel's name and ID. A section without a pixel shows a dash, and one pixel selected in all three sections appears three times.

## Step 3. Map events

To send conversion events back to TikTok for campaign optimization, you need to configure event mapping in the **Events names** section. This allows Adapty to automatically send subscription events when users perform actions in your app.

In the **Events names** section, toggle on the events you want to track in TikTok Ads Manager. For each enabled event, select the corresponding TikTok event from the dropdown or set a custom one. By default, Adapty maps Adapty events to TikTok's standard events.

Click **Save** to apply your event mapping configuration.

<ZoomImage id="ua-meta-events.webp" width="900px" />

## Additional configuration

### Additional parameters

The **Additional parameter** field allows you to add custom data points for analysis outside Adapty. This is useful when you need to pass specific campaign or user data to external analytics tools or attribution partners.

In the **Additional parameter** field, enter any custom data you want to include with your attribution tracking. The additional parameter will be included in all attribution data sent to TikTok and can be used for advanced campaign analysis and optimization.

For example, if you're running multiple variations of the same campaign, you could add `variant=A` or `variant=B` to distinguish between different creative approaches.

:::important
Additional parameters change the **Click link** you paste in TikTok Ads Manager. If you have already copied this link there and added a custom parameter later, ensure you copy and paste an updated click link that contains the custom parameter.
:::

### Settings

The **Settings** tab controls how Adapty matches user actions to your TikTok Ads campaigns. These settings determine the time windows for both deterministic and probabilistic attribution matching.

To configure them, go to the **Settings** tab in your campaign configuration. Here you'll find two main settings to adjust:

- **Deterministic matching window**: This uses exact device identifiers (like IDFA on iOS or Advertising ID on Android) to match users to campaigns with high precision. Set this to 168 hours (7 days) for maximum attribution accuracy - this is the default and recommended value. When a user clicks your TikTok ad and installs your app within this window, Adapty can definitively attribute the install to that specific ad click using device identifiers.

- **Probabilistic matching window**: This uses statistical modeling and device fingerprinting to match users when deterministic matching isn't possible. Set this to 6 hours for most campaigns - this is the default and works well for most use cases. For campaigns with high click volumes, you can reduce this to 1-2 hours. For users who can't be matched deterministically (due to privacy settings or other factors), Adapty uses probabilistic matching within this shorter window.

Click **Save** to apply your settings.

<ZoomImage id="ua-meta-attribution-settings.webp" width="900px" />

### Revenue override

If you track trial events and want TikTok to attribute revenue to them, use the **Revenue override** section. It appears when the **Trial started** event is enabled. The override applies to **Trial started** only — **Trial converted** and **Subscription started** always carry their actual revenue.

For each trial event target, set the percentage of the subscription price to report as revenue. For example, at 30%, Adapty sends 30% of the subscription price as the conversion value for trial events.

To add an override:

1. Enable **Trial started** in the **Events names** section.
2. In **Revenue override**, click **Add override**.
3. Select the target event and enter a revenue percentage (0–100).
4. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="revenue-override.webp" width="500px" />

### Qualified trial

A trial qualifies once it survives a given number of hours without being cancelled. Use the **Qualified trial** section to hold trial events until they qualify, so that TikTok receives only the trials users kept.

For each trial event target, set the number of hours to wait. Adapty counts the hours from the trial start and sends the event when the deadline passes. If the user turns off auto-renewal before the deadline, Adapty never sends the event. If the user turns auto-renewal back on before the deadline, Adapty sends the event as planned.

To hold a trial event until it qualifies:

1. Enable **Trial started** in the **Events names** section.
2. In **Qualified trial**, click **Add event**.
3. Select the target event and enter the number of hours to wait, from 1 to 24.
4. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="qualified-trial.webp" width="700px" alt="Qualified trial section with a target event held for two hours in a campaign configuration" />

Adapty sends the qualified event with the original trial start time, so TikTok reports it on the day the trial started.

:::important
Adapty matches qualified trials by their App Store or Google Play transaction, so the hold applies to in-app trials only. Trial events from web purchases, such as [FunnelFox](ua-funnelfox), are sent immediately.
:::

**Revenue override** and **Qualified trial** work independently, and you can apply both to the same trial event. Revenue override changes the value TikTok receives; Qualified trial changes when TikTok receives it. With both, Adapty holds the event until the trial qualifies, then sends it with the overridden revenue. Neither is recommended over the other — test what works for your campaigns.

### Send all events

By default, Adapty only reports the events of users who came from one of your TikTok campaigns. The **Send all events** option reports every install and transaction instead, including those from organic and non-attributed users.

We don't recommend it unless your campaigns send TikTok too few conversions to optimize on, because mixing organic and non-attributed events with your campaign events makes TikTok's optimization less precise.

If you decide to turn it on, in the campaign settings select **Send all events (forward organic/non-attributed events to this pixel)** and click **Save**.
