With the TikTok for Business integration of Adapty Attribution, you can track and optimize your TikTok campaigns.

:::tip
See our [guide on setting up ads in TikTok for Business](tiktok-create-campaign).
:::

## Step 1. Connect your TikTok account

1. Go to **Integrations > TikTok Ads** from the left sidebar and click **Continue with TikTok**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-connect-tiktok.webp" width="700px" />

2. Sign in using your TikTok account and click **Continue**.

3. Review the requested permissions and click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-tiktok-sign.webp" width="500px" />

After that, all your ad accounts will be added to Adapty Attribution. You can proceed with adding campaigns.

## Step 2. Add campaigns

A single campaign can cover the App Store, Google Play, and your web funnel at once. Two pieces make the connection work:

- **Tracking link**: Goes into your ad. When a user taps the ad, Adapty records the click, sends the user on to the destination, and later attributes the install and purchases to that ad. Each destination gets its own link.
- **Pixel**: Selected in the campaign, from the pixels in your TikTok account. Carries those conversions back to TikTok, so TikTok can optimize the campaign on real purchases. One pixel can serve all three destinations, or each destination can have its own.

To add a TikTok for Business campaign to Adapty Attribution and track how your TikTok ads work in Adapty:

1. Switch to the app that you want to advertise.

2. Open the **Web Campaigns** tab and click **Create configuration**. This configuration only counts the installs of the app that you switched to.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-new-campaign-tiktok.webp" width="500px" />

3. In the **General** tab, expand the **iOS** and/or **Android** section and paste App Store and/or Google Play application URLs.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-url.webp" width="900px" />

4. Copy the **Click link** field value. Then, in TikTok Ads Manager, when creating your ad, paste this value in the **Destination URL** field, as described in [Add ad content](tiktok-create-campaign#step-5-add-ad-content). This will allow Adapty to connect installs and purchases to ads in TikTok. Adapty only connects installs to your ad when users go straight from it to the store through this link.

   <ZoomImage id="ua-lnk.webp" width="900px" />

5. (Optional) To send the conversion events back to TikTok, associate your TikTok pixels with this configuration:
   1. From the **Pixel** dropdown, select the pixel that your campaign uses in TikTok Ads Manager. If you don't have a pixel yet, [create one in TikTok](tiktok-create-campaign#step-2-create-a-pixel).
   2. To verify the connection, click **Send test event**.

6. If your ads lead to a web funnel, expand the **Web funnel** section of the configuration:
   1. (Optional) From the **Pixel** dropdown, select the pixel for web purchases. TikTok only learns about purchases on your funnel through this pixel.
   2. To install the [Adapty web pixel](ua-web-pixel), paste the **Pixel snippet** on every page of your funnel.
   3. To [tag your ad URLs](ua-tag-ad-urls), copy the **Ad URL parameters**.

   If your funnel runs on [FunnelFox](ua-funnelfox), skip the snippet. Adapty doesn't send FunnelFox purchases to TikTok. To send them, set up the [TikTok Pixel integration](https://funnelfox.com/docs/integrations/tracking/tiktok-pixel) in FunnelFox.

To set up your pixels correctly:

- **Match the pixels to your campaigns**: If one campaign covers the App Store, Google Play, and your web funnel, select the same pixel in all three sections. If you run a separate campaign for each, select a separate pixel for each, so that every campaign optimizes on its own purchases. The **Web Campaigns** list shows the pixels of each configuration in the **Pixel (iOS)**, **Pixel (Android)**, and **Pixel (Web)** columns.
- **Edit the existing configuration to switch pixels**: Running ads keep the click link of their configuration, so their conversions go to that configuration's pixels. To switch the pixel of running ads, select another pixel in that configuration. A new configuration doesn't change where their conversions go.
- **Fill in the Event source URL**: If you connected your pixel to your website in TikTok, enter the website's address in the **Event source URL** field of the configuration. Otherwise, TikTok may mark the conversions from Adapty as coming from an unknown website. This field doesn't change the address of your tracking links.

## Step 3. Map events

Adapty sends the events that you select, such as started trials, to the pixels of the configuration. TikTok uses these events to find people who are likely to start a trial or buy a subscription.

To select the events, in the **Events mapping** section of the configuration:

1. Turn on each event that Adapty should send to TikTok.
2. For each event, select a TikTok event from the dropdown, or type a custom one. By default, Adapty selects TikTok's standard events. **Install** can only be sent as **Lead**.
3. Click **Save**.

## Additional configuration

### Additional parameters

The **Additional parameter** field allows you to add custom data points for analysis outside Adapty. This is useful when you need to pass specific campaign or user data to external analytics tools or attribution partners.

In the **Additional parameter** field, enter any custom data you want to include with your attribution tracking. The additional parameter will be included in all attribution data sent to TikTok and can be used for advanced campaign analysis and optimization.

For example, if you're running multiple variations of the same campaign, you could add `variant=A` or `variant=B` to distinguish between different creative approaches.

:::important
Additional parameters change the **Click link** you paste in TikTok Ads Manager. If you have already copied this link there and added a custom parameter later, ensure you copy and paste an updated click link that contains the custom parameter.
:::

### Settings

The **Settings** tab of a configuration controls how Adapty matches installs to clicks on your TikTok ads. Adapty uses one of two methods:

| Method | Installs | How Adapty credits the install | Time limit between the click and the install |
|---|---|---|---|
| Match by click ID | Android installs with a click ID | To the click with the ID that Google Play passes to your app | 168 hours (7 days) by default, up to 720 hours |
| Probabilistic match | iOS installs, and Android installs without a click ID | To the most likely recent click from the same IP address range and operating system | 6 hours by default, up to 24 hours |

For a probabilistic match, Adapty selects the click that scores best on time since the click, device language, and country. Adapty doesn't use the IDFA or the Google Advertising ID, so probabilistic matching covers iOS users who don't allow tracking.

Adapty can't match an install probabilistically if the device switches networks between the click and the install, for example from Wi-Fi to cellular. On networks that many devices share, such as mobile carrier networks, Adapty matches fewer installs probabilistically.

To change the time limits, in the **New user attribution** section of the **Settings** tab, enter each limit in hours:

- **Deterministic Matching Window**: For a match by click ID.
- **Probabilistic Matching Window**: For a probabilistic match. A longer limit matches more installs, but more of those matches are wrong. For campaigns with high click volumes, reduce it to 1–2 hours. If you set more than 6 hours, clicks more than 6 hours before the install must also match the device model and OS version of the install.

Keep the switches of both fields turned on. Without probabilistic matching, Adapty can't match iOS installs from the links of this configuration.

### Revenue override

By default, Adapty sends the **Trial started** event to TikTok without a revenue value, because no payment happens when a trial starts. With a revenue override, Adapty sends a value that you set: a percentage of the subscription price. For example, at 30%, Adapty sends 30% of the subscription price. **Trial converted** and **Subscription started** always have their actual revenue.

A revenue override changes the value that TikTok receives for a trial. A [qualified trial](#qualified-trial) changes when TikTok receives it. You can use both for the same event.

To report revenue to TikTok for started trials:

1. In the **Events mapping** section, turn on the **Trial started** event. The **Revenue override** section appears.
2. In the **Revenue override** section, click **Add event**.
3. Select the TikTok event that you mapped to **Trial started**, such as **StartTrial**.
4. Enter the percentage of the subscription price to send, from 0 to 100.
5. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="revenue-override.webp" width="500px" />

### Qualified trial

:::important
Qualified trial reports require [App Store Server Notifications](enable-app-store-server-notifications) for iOS and [Real-time Developer Notifications](enable-real-time-developer-notifications-rtdn) for Android. The App Store and Google Play report trial cancellations to Adapty through these channels.
:::

By default, Adapty sends each started trial to TikTok immediately, even if the user cancels it soon after. With a qualified trial, TikTok only counts the trials that users keep — if the autorenewal isn't disabled a set number of hours after the trial start. Adapty sends the event with the original trial start time, so TikTok reports it on the day the trial started. If the event also has a revenue override, Adapty sends it with the overridden revenue.

To only send TikTok the trials that users keep:

1. In the **Events mapping** section, turn on the **Trial started** event.
2. In the **Qualified trial** section, click **Add event**.
3. Select the TikTok event that you mapped to **Trial started**, such as **StartTrial**.
4. Enter the number of hours to wait, from 1 to 24. Adapty sends the event about 45 minutes after the wait ends.
5. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="qualified-trial.webp" width="700px" alt="Qualified trial section with a target event held for two hours in a campaign configuration" />

If the user turns auto-renewal back on before the wait ends, Adapty sends the event as planned. Qualified trials only apply to trials from the App Store and Google Play.

#### Qualified trials vs. the Trial still active event

Adapty has two ways to only report the trials that users keep. For TikTok, use a qualified trial: Adapty Attribution can't send the [Trial still active](events#synthetic-events) event to TikTok. If another integration, such as AppsFlyer, also sends trials to TikTok, TikTok receives the same trial more than once.

| | Qualified trial | **Trial still active** event |
|---|---|---|
| Where you set it up | In Adapty Attribution, in a Meta, TikTok, or Google Ads configuration | In Adapty, in integrations such as AppsFlyer, Adjust, Firebase, or the webhook |
| What it does | Delays the **Trial started** event | Adds a separate event after **Trial started** |
| Wait time | 1 to 24 hours | 1 hour to 90 days |
| If the user turns off auto-renewal before the wait ends | Adapty doesn't send the event | Adapty doesn't send the event |
| Sent to TikTok by Adapty Attribution | Yes | No |

### Send all events

By default, Adapty only reports the events of users who came from one of your TikTok campaigns. The **Send all events** option reports every install and transaction instead, including those from organic and non-attributed users. Purchases from FunnelFox funnels are the exception: Adapty only uses them in Adapty Attribution analytics. To send them to TikTok, set up the [TikTok Pixel integration](https://funnelfox.com/docs/integrations/tracking/tiktok-pixel) in FunnelFox.

We don't recommend it unless your campaigns send TikTok too few conversions to optimize on, because mixing organic and non-attributed events with your campaign events makes TikTok's optimization less precise.

If you decide to turn it on, in the configuration settings select **Send all events (forward organic/non-attributed events to this pixel)** and click **Save**.

## Read TikTok Ads data correctly in Adapty Attribution

To compare TikTok spend with your installs and revenue in [Adapty Attribution analytics](ua-analytics):

- **Expect recent spend to change**: TikTok keeps adjusting spend, impressions, and clicks for recent days. Every 4 hours, Adapty refreshes these numbers for the last 3 days, including today.
- **Expect daily spend and revenue to use different time zones**: Adapty Attribution shows TikTok spend by day in the time zone of your ad account. It shows installs and revenue by day in UTC. If the two time zones differ, daily spend and daily revenue cover different hours. To show installs and revenue in another time zone, contact [Adapty support](mailto:support@adapty.io).
