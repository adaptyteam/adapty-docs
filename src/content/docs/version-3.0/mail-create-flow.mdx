In Adapty Mail, a **flow** connects a **trigger** to a **campaign**, and a **user segment** decides who receives it.

A trigger is the event that starts a flow. Each trigger has its own tab, where you pair segments with the campaigns they receive, in order of priority.

This guide covers how to add, reorder, edit, and remove those segments. See [Flows](mail-flows) for a higher-level overview of the concepts involved.

## Add a segment to a flow

1. In Adapty Mail, go to **Flows** and open the trigger you want to configure.
2. Click **Create** to open the dialog.

   <ZoomImage id="create-flow-mail.webp" width="500px" alt="Create flow dialog with Segment, Content type, and Campaign fields" />

3. In the dialog:
   - **Segment**: Pick a segment, or **All Users** as a catch-all.
   - **Content type**: **Campaign** for a single campaign, or **A/B Test** to compare multiple — see [A/B testing](mail-ab-testing).
   - **Campaign**: Select the campaign to send. The list offers only the campaigns written for this trigger — see [purpose](mail-create-campaign).
   <ZoomImage id="mail-flow-details.webp" width="500px" alt="Flow details view with a prioritized list of segment-to-campaign rows" />

4. Click **Save**.

The flow goes live the moment you save. Users who hit the trigger and match the segment start receiving the campaign from that point on, with the first email arriving after its [**Delay**](mail-flows#email-delivery-timing).

## Change priority

To reorder, drag the handle on the left of any row.

**All Users**, if present, must have the lowest priority — be the final entry for the trigger. Otherwise the flow won't save.

## Change what a segment receives

Click **Change content** on a row to reopen the dialog with its current values pre-filled. You can change the segment, content type, and campaign, then click **Save** to apply.

A segment paired with an A/B test can only be edited while the test is in the **draft** state. Once the test is launched, its content is locked until you finish it.

## Remove a segment from a flow

Open the row's actions menu and click **Delete**. There's no confirmation dialog — the row is removed immediately.

- **Segments paired with a campaign**: Can be removed at any time.
- **Segments paired with a live A/B test**: Can't be removed. Finish the test first using **Finish A/B test**.

:::note
Removing a segment stops new users from entering the sequence. Users already mid-sequence may continue receiving their scheduled emails — there's no automatic cancellation.
:::
