In Adapty Mail, a **flow** sends a campaign or an A/B test to one **segment** of your users. The flow's **purpose** is the moment of the customer lifecycle it covers, such as an expired subscription or a failed payment. A purpose can have several flows, one for each segment, in order of priority.

This guide covers how to create, reorder, edit, and delete flows. See [Flows](mail-flows) for an overview of purposes, segments, and priority.

:::tip
An AI assistant connected to the [Adapty Mail MCP server](mail-mcp) can create and reorder flows for you. Creating a flow starts sending, so read the assistant's plan before you approve it. The [Adapty Mail skill](mail-skill) shows every change before it saves one.
:::

## Create a flow

1. In Adapty Mail, go to **Flows**. Open the **Marketing** tab for a marketing flow, or the **Transactional** tab for service messages.
2. Click **Create**.
3. In the dialog, select the purpose of the flow, then click **Continue**. The purpose decides what starts the flow and which campaigns it can send.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-flow-purpose-picker.webp" width="400px" alt="The New marketing flow dialog with the five marketing purposes and Never purchased selected" />

4. On the **New Flow** page, fill in the fields:
   - **Segment**: Pick a segment, or **All Users** to reach everyone. A segment that already has a flow for this purpose shows **(has a flow already)** and can't be picked.
   - **Content type**: **Campaign** to send one campaign to everyone in the segment, or **A/B Test** to split the segment between the campaigns of a test. See [A/B testing](mail-ab-testing).
   - **Campaign** or **A/B Test**: Select what the flow sends. The list offers only the campaigns or draft A/B tests written for this purpose — see [purpose](mail-create-campaign). To write a new one, click **Create campaign** or **Create A/B test** at the bottom of the list. Adapty Mail keeps what you entered on this page and brings you back to it.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-flow-create.webp" width="600px" alt="The New Flow page with the Never purchased purpose, the Trial users segment, and a campaign selected" />

5. Click **Create**.

The flow goes live as soon as you create it. Users who hit the trigger and match the segment start receiving the campaign from that point on, with the first email arriving after its [**Delay**](mail-flows#email-delivery-timing). A draft A/B test starts when you create the flow.

A new flow goes after the other flows of its purpose. A flow with **All Users** always stays last.

## Change the order of flows

When a purpose has several flows, Adapty Mail evaluates them in order of priority and starts the first flow whose segment a profile is in. The **Priority** column of the **Flows** page shows the order.

To change the order:

1. On the **Flows** page, click **Change order**. The button is active when at least one purpose has two flows with a number.
2. In the **Purpose** dropdown, select the purpose.
3. Drag the flows into the order you need.
4. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="mail-flow-change-order.webp" width="400px" alt="The Change order dialog for the Never purchased purpose, with two numbered flows and the All Users flow last" />

A flow with **All Users** has no number and always stays last, because it matches every profile.

## Change what a flow sends

1. On the **Flows** page, click the name in the **Content** column, or open the row menu and select **Edit flow**.
2. Change the **Content type** and the **Campaign** or **A/B Test**.
3. Click **Save**.

<ZoomImage id="mail-flow-row-menu.webp" width="700px" alt="The Flows page with the row menu of a flow open: Edit flow, View in analytics, Finish A/B test, and Delete" />

The new content starts at once. What the flow sent before stops for new users.

The purpose and the segment of a flow don't change. To send to another segment, delete the flow and create another one.

While a flow runs a live A/B test, its content is locked. To send something else, [finish the test](mail-ab-testing#finish-the-test) first.

## Delete a flow

1. On the **Flows** page, open the row menu of the flow and select **Delete**.
2. In the **Delete flow** dialog, click **Delete**.

When you delete a flow:

- **New users**: The flow stops taking them. The flows after it in the purpose move up a place.
- **Users already in the flow**: They keep receiving the rest of their scheduled emails.
- **A live A/B test**: If the flow runs one, Adapty Mail finishes it.
