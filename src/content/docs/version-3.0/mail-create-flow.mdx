In Adapty Mail, a **flow** sends a campaign or an A/B test to one **segment** of your users. The flow's **purpose** is the moment of the customer lifecycle it covers, such as an expired subscription or a failed payment. A purpose can have several flows, one for each segment, in order of priority. The purpose also sets the flow's **trigger**: what starts the flow for a user.

This guide covers how to create, reorder, edit, and delete flows. See [Flows](mail-flows) for an overview of purposes, segments, and priority.

:::tip
An AI assistant connected to the [Adapty Mail MCP server](mail-mcp) can create and reorder flows for you. Creating a flow starts sending, so read the assistant's plan before you approve it. The [Adapty Mail skill](mail-skill) shows every change before it saves one.
:::

## Create a flow

:::important
A flow has no draft. It starts sending as soon as you click **Create**, to every user in its segment who hits the trigger. Review the campaign first, and [test the flow](mail-testing) with a test user.
:::

1. In Adapty Mail, go to **Flows**. Open the **Marketing** tab for a marketing flow, or the **Transactional** tab for service messages.

   For the **Double opt-in** purpose, turn on [double opt-in](mail-double-opt-in) instead. The setting creates the flow, and turning it off deletes every Double opt-in flow, including ones you created here.

2. Click **Create**.
3. In the **New marketing flow** or **New transactional flow** dialog, select the purpose of the flow, then click **Continue**. The purpose decides what starts the flow and which campaigns it can send.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-flow-purpose-picker.webp" width="400px" alt="The New marketing flow dialog with the five marketing purposes and Never purchased selected" />

4. On the **New Flow** page, fill in the fields:
   - **Segment**: Pick a segment, or **All Users** to reach everyone. A segment that already has a flow for this purpose shows **(has a flow already)** and can't be picked. To change what that segment receives, [change what its flow sends](#change-what-a-flow-sends). To make a new segment, click **Create segment** at the bottom of the list. When you create a segment, campaign, or A/B test from these lists, Adapty Mail keeps what you entered on this page and brings you back to it.
   - **Content type**: **Campaign** to send one campaign to everyone in the segment, or **A/B Test** to split the segment between the campaigns of a test. See [A/B testing](mail-ab-testing).
   - **Campaign** or **A/B Test**: Select what the flow sends. The list offers only the campaigns with this [campaign purpose](mail-create-campaign), or the draft A/B tests with that purpose. A test you pick runs on this flow's segment. To write a new one, click **Create campaign** or **Create A/B test** at the bottom of the list.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-flow-create.webp" width="600px" alt="The New Flow page with the Never purchased purpose, the EU users segment, and a campaign selected" />

5. Click **Create**.

The flow goes live as soon as you create it. Users who hit the trigger and are in the segment start receiving the campaign. The first email arrives after its [**Delay**](mail-flows#email-delivery-timing).

If the flow sends an A/B test, the test goes live when you create the flow.

A new flow goes after the other numbered flows of its purpose. If a flow before it has a broader segment, that flow takes every user the new one would get, and the new flow sends nothing. To fix this, [move the new flow up](#change-the-order-of-flows).

## Change the order of flows

When a purpose has several flows, Adapty Mail evaluates them in order of priority and starts the first flow whose segment the user is in. The **Priority** column of the **Flows** page shows the order.

To change the order:

1. On the **Flows** page, open the tab of the flows, then click **Change order**. The button is active when a purpose on that tab has at least two flows besides its **All Users** flow.
2. In the **Purpose** dropdown, select the purpose.
3. Drag the flows into the order you need.
4. Click **Save**. The button is active once you've moved a flow.

<ZoomImage id="mail-flow-change-order.webp" width="400px" alt="The Change order dialog for the Never purchased purpose, with two numbered flows and the All Users flow last" />

In the **Priority** column, a flow with **All Users** shows **Last**. It always stays last, because every user is in its segment.

## Change what a flow sends

1. On the **Flows** page, find the flow. In its row, click the campaign or test name in the **Content** column, or open the row menu and select **Edit flow**.

   <ZoomImage id="mail-flow-row-menu.webp" width="700px" alt="The Flows page with the row menu of a flow open: Edit flow, View in analytics, Finish A/B test, and Delete" />

2. Change the **Content type** and the **Campaign** or **A/B Test**.
3. Click **Save**. **Save** appears once the content differs from what the flow sends.

Users who hit the trigger after you save get the new campaign or A/B test. Users already in the previous sequence keep receiving its emails. The numbers in the flow's row start again from the new content. To see the earlier numbers, use [Analytics](mail-analytics).

While a flow runs a live A/B test, its content is locked and **Save** doesn't appear. To send something else, [finish the test](mail-ab-testing#finish-the-test) first.

## Send a flow to another segment

You can't change the purpose or the segment of a flow. To send to another segment, replace the flow:

1. [Create a flow](#create-a-flow) with the new segment.
2. [Move it](#change-the-order-of-flows) to the place directly before the old flow.
3. [Delete](#delete-a-flow) the old flow.

In this order, no user falls through to another flow in between. If the old flow runs a live A/B test, deleting the flow finishes the test.

## Delete a flow

1. On the **Flows** page, open the row menu of the flow and select **Delete**.
2. In the **Delete flow** dialog, click **Delete**.

When you delete a flow:

- **Users who hit the trigger later**: They enter the next flow of the purpose whose segment they're in, such as **All Users**, or none if they're in no flow's segment.
- **Priority**: The flows after it move up one place.
- **Users already in the flow**: They keep receiving the rest of their scheduled emails.
- **A live A/B test**: If the flow runs one, Adapty Mail finishes it.
