Each email links to a checkout page in the browser, where the purchase takes place. To create one, in Adapty Mail go to **Email Destinations**, select **Web Paywall**, and click **Create**. There are two options:

| | Generate with AI | Host your own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who builds the page | Adapty Mail, in the bundled web paywall builder | You |
| Payment provider | Stripe only | Any |
| Project | Must be linked to an Adapty app | Any |
| Your work | Pick plans, then review and publish | Build the page, read the recipient from the URL, report purchases back |

:::note
Adapty's core product has its own web paywalls. They're a different feature — nothing here applies to them.
:::

## Generate with AI

:::warning
AI generation needs a project **linked to an Adapty app** — one Adapty created for you the first time you opened Mail from the Adapty dashboard. If you created the project yourself in Adapty Mail, on a standalone account or your Adapty account, host your own paywall.
:::

### What a generated paywall looks like

Generation builds four screens, and a recipient clicking from an email meets them in order:

1. **Email** — a heading, a subheading, and an email field. Adapty Mail prefills it from the link, so campaign traffic can go straight on; anyone arriving another way types it.
2. **Paywall** — the sales page, below.
3. **Payment success** or **Payment failed** — one or the other, never both.

AI writes every part of the paywall screen from your brand:

| Section | What it holds |
| --- | --- |
| Headline and subheadline | The opening pitch |
| Offer badge | Urgency text, with no invented prices |
| Hero image | Generated from your brand |
| Plans | A title, an offer timer, and the plans you picked |
| Benefits | 3–6 cards |
| Features | 3–8, each with a title and subtitle |
| Social proof | A line of proof text, and 3–5 reviews |
| FAQ | 3–6 questions and answers |
| Guarantee | A money-back or satisfaction line |

A CTA and countdown stay pinned at the top throughout. **Payment success** shows a generated image and next steps; **Payment failed** invites another attempt and keeps the checkout state.

## Use your own hosted paywall

With this approach, Adapty Mail sends recipients to a page you host. The URL includes [placeholders](#personalize-your-url-with-placeholders-and-hooks) that forward user information. Everything after the click — the offer, the payment, the personalization — happens on your side, with any payment provider.

1. Give the destination a **Name** — you'll pick it by that name when you attach it to a campaign.
2. Paste your hosted paywall URL into **URL**. It must use HTTPS, and it can carry [placeholders](#personalize-your-url-with-placeholders-and-hooks) for the values that differ per recipient.
3. Click **Save**. The destination is then available to campaigns.

### Personalize your URL with placeholders and hooks

Use placeholders and hooks to personalize your paywall URL with values unique to each recipient. [Placeholders](mail-destinations#placeholders) lists the full set and the rules.

### What your page has to do

Adapty Mail doesn't do anything after the redirect. The following actions are your responsibility:

- **Identify the visitor before checkout starts**: Read the profile ID or email from the query string and attach it to the purchase, so the payment lands on the customer the email was sent to.
- **Report the purchase back**: Adapty Mail never sees a payment taken on your own provider, and a purchase it doesn't know about counts as neither a conversion nor attributed revenue in [Analytics](mail-analytics) — worse, the rest of the sequence keeps going out to someone who already bought. Send it through whichever path already carries your data: the [Adapty integration](mail-collect-emails), or a [transaction event](mail-send-data-via-api#send-transaction-events) on the Adapty Mail API.

### Test the paywall before launching

**Send test email** proves the email renders and your page takes payment, but **Sandbox** isn't selectable for a paywall added by URL — the modal reports *"This paywall has no sandbox products, so only production is available."* So a test goes to your live page with live products: use a real card and refund it, or point the URL at your own test environment while you verify the wiring. See [Test your setup](mail-testing#test-the-email-and-checkout-on-their-own).

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Solution |
|---|---|
| Checkout link doesn't open | Verify the paywall is published in the web paywall builder |
| User not identified at checkout | Check the URL carries a placeholder for the source your data comes from. A placeholder for the wrong source resolves to an empty string, so the parameter arrives blank |
| Purchase not attributed to email | Attribution follows the click on the email's CTA, so the recipient has to reach checkout through that link, and the purchase has to reach Adapty Mail on the same profile. A purchase already attributed to an earlier email keeps that attribution |
