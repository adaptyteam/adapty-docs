## What "paywall" means

Adapty uses **paywall** for two different things:

- **A paywall** — product data that a [hand-coded purchase screen](ios-implement-paywalls-manually) requests from Adapty. Your app fetches this data from a [paywall placement](placements) before [asking Adapty to conduct the purchase](making-purchases).
- **A paywall screen** — a screen you build in the [Flow & Paywall Builder](adapty-flow-builder), inside a flow. Adapty draws it for you. It isn't a paywall: its products belong to the flow, and the flow is served from a flow placement.

So "the paywall" can mean the products you configured, or the screen a user sees. When it matters, say **paywall** for the first and **paywall screen** for the second.

You may also see **paywall** used for a screen built in the [legacy Paywall Builder](adapty-paywall-builder). Those paywalls keep working, but that builder stops accepting new ones on October 1, 2026.
