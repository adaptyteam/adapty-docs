A **custom paywall** is the product configuration that feeds a paywall screen you build in your own code. You build and maintain the screen yourself, and Adapty runs the purchase. To compare custom paywalls with flows, which Adapty designs and renders for you, see [Flows & paywalls](flows-and-paywalls).

<CustomDocCardList ids={['create-paywall', 'paywall-metrics']} />

To show a custom paywall in your app:

1. [Create the custom paywall](create-paywall) and save your products in it: their order, their promotional offers, and an optional [remote config](customize-paywall-with-remote-config) for text and media.
2. Serve it from a [paywall placement](placements).
3. Fetch the custom paywall for its products, and call `makePurchase` to complete the purchase — see [Implement custom paywalls](ios-implement-paywalls-manually).
