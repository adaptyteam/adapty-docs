Tracking links let you measure where your users come from and connect installs to ad campaigns.
When someone clicks your ad, Adapty records the click and later matches it with the install event sent by the SDK. This way, you can see which channels, campaigns, ad sets, and ads drive the most revenue on your [Analytics page](ua-analytics).

You can create two types of tracking links:

- **One link** — a universal link that automatically detects the user’s platform, records the click, and redirects them to the App Store or Google Play.

A tracking link opens your App Store or Google Play page, even for a user who already has your app. The click doesn't change which campaign Adapty credits the existing install to. If the user deletes your app and installs it again after the click, Adapty counts the reinstall as a new install and can credit it to the click.

Adapty generates the links for [Meta Ads](ua-facebook#step-2-add-campaigns) and [TikTok for Business](ua-tiktok#step-2-add-campaigns) campaigns, and a tracking template for [Google Ads](google-ads-search-pmax-campaigns#create-the-configuration-in-adapty-attribution) campaigns. For every other ad network, [create a custom tracking link](#custom-tracking-links) with the **Custom** integration in the Dashboard.

## Create tracking links

To create a tracking link:

- **Platform-specific links** — a separate link for each store. They can lead users to customized store listings, which match your campaign.
- **One Link** — a single link for both stores, matched to the user's platform at click time.

One tracking link page holds the links for every store you configure, so you never set up a second one per store. For greater flexibility, we recommend platform-specific links.

## Custom tracking links

:::important
Don't create custom tracking links for these networks:

- **Meta Ads, TikTok for Business, and Google Ads**: Use their integrations instead. With them, Adapty imports ad spend from each network and sends conversions back to it.
- **Apple Search Ads**: Adapty gets the attribution data from Apple without a link. To manage these campaigns, use [Adapty Ads Manager](adapty-ads-manager).
:::

To create a custom tracking link:

1. In the Adapty Attribution dashboard, open **Integrations** and select the **Custom** card in the **Network** group.

<ZoomImage id="custom-tracking-link.webp" width="700px" alt="Integrations page in Adapty Attribution with the Custom card highlighted in the Network group" />

2. Click **Create link**.

<ZoomImage id="new-tracking-link.webp" width="700px" />

2. Select your app from the list and click **Next**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-choose-app.webp" width="700px" />

3. Fill in the link parameters to match it with the campaign and ad you want to track. 

| Parameter         | Description                                                                                   |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Name**          | The internal tracking link name.                                                              |
| **Channel**       | The source of traffic, such as Reddit or Snapchat. Used to group campaigns in analytics.      |
| **Campaign ID**   | The unique identifier of the campaign in your ad platform.                                    |
| **Campaign name** | The readable name of the campaign.                                                            |
| **Adset ID**      | The unique identifier of the ad set (ad group) in your ad platform.                           |
| **Adset Name**    | The name of the ad set.                                                                       |
| **Ad ID**         | The unique identifier of the individual ad creative.                                          |
| **Ad name**       | The name of the ad creative or variation.                                                     |

4. By default, you are creating a One Link. It automatically detects the user’s platform and redirects them to the App Store or Google Play after tracking the click.

If you prefer to use separate redirect URLs for each platform, deselect the **One Link** checkbox and provide platform-specific store links manually.

<ZoomImage id="one-link.webp" width="700px" />

5. Click **Create**.
6. Open your tracking link page and copy the **Click link** from one of the sections:
   - **One link** – use this link to track clicks and automatically redirect users to the correct store.
   - **iOS link** or **Android link** — optional platform-specific versions if you want separate links for each store.

:::tip
You can also set additional link parameters to [work with deferred data](ua-deferred-data). For example, you can implement deferred deep linking.
:::

<ZoomImage id="click-link.webp" width="700px" />

7. Go to your ad platform and paste the link to your ad as an ad destination URL.

Now, app installations will be matched with the ads and campaigns they are coming from, so you can measure the campaign effectiveness on the **Analytics** page.
