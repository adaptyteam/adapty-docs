:::important
Tracking links help Adapty attribute app installs. If your ad sends people to a web funnel, [tag its URL with Adapty's parameters](ua-tag-ad-urls) instead.
:::

Your ad's destination is usually your App Store or Google Play page. That page belongs to the store, not you, so it cannot report clicks to Adapty.

Use a tracking link as the destination instead. Adapty captures the campaign, ad set, and ad before redirecting the user to the store — then matches that click to the install the SDK reports. Your [Adapty Attribution analytics](ua-analytics) show which ads drive revenue.

Adapty generates the links for [Meta Ads](ua-facebook#step-2-add-campaigns) and [TikTok for Business](ua-tiktok#step-2-add-campaigns) campaigns. For every other ad network, [create a custom tracking link](#custom-tracking-links) with the **Custom** integration in the Dashboard.

## Tracking link types

Adapty generates two kinds of tracking link:

- **Platform-specific links** — a separate link for each store. They can lead users to customized store listings, which match your campaign.
- **One Link** — a single link for both stores, matched to the user's platform at click time.

One tracking link page holds the links for every store you configure, so you never set up a second one per store. For greater flexibility, we recommend platform-specific links.

## Custom tracking links

:::note
Apple Search Ads needs no tracking link. Adapty receives Apple Search Ads attribution without one, and you manage those campaigns in [Adapty Ads Manager](adapty-ads-manager).
:::

To create a custom tracking link:

1. In the Adapty Attribution dashboard, open **Integrations** and select the **Custom** card in the **Network** group.

<ZoomImage id="custom-tracking-link.webp" width="700px" alt="Integrations page in Adapty Attribution with the Custom card highlighted in the Network group" />

2. Click **Create link**.

<ZoomImage id="new-tracking-link.webp" width="700px" />

3. Select your app from the list and click **Next**.

<ZoomImage id="ua-choose-app.webp" width="700px" />

4. Fill in the link parameters to match the link to a particular campaign and ad.

| Parameter         | Description                                                                                   |
|-------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Name**          | The internal tracking link name.                                                              |
| **Channel**       | The source of traffic, such as Meta, Reddit, or TikTok. Used to group campaigns in analytics. |
| **Campaign ID**   | The unique identifier of the campaign in your ad platform.                                    |
| **Campaign name** | The readable name of the campaign.                                                            |
| **Ad set ID**     | The unique identifier of the ad set (ad group) in your ad platform.                           |
| **Ad set name**   | The name of the ad set.                                                                       |
| **Ad ID**         | The unique identifier of the individual ad creative.                                          |
| **Ad name**       | The name of the ad creative or variation.                                                     |

5. By default, you are creating a One Link. To add [platform-specific links](#tracking-link-types) instead, deselect the **One Link** checkbox.

<ZoomImage id="one-link.webp" width="700px" />

6. Click **Create**.
7. Open your tracking link page. In the section for your [link type](#tracking-link-types), copy the **Click link** value.

:::tip
You can also set additional link parameters to [work with deferred data](ua-deferred-data). For example, you can implement deferred deep linking.
:::

<ZoomImage id="click-link.webp" width="700px" />

8. Go to your ad platform and use the link as the ad's destination URL.
