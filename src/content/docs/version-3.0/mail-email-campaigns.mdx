A campaign in Adapty Mail is a complete multi-email sequence — copy, design, hero images, and timing settings — generated for your app in one pass. A campaign on its own doesn't send: it saves as a **draft**, and an Adapty Mail [flow](mail-create-flow) is what gives it an audience. The [purpose](mail-purposes) of a campaign is the situation its emails are written for, such as an expired subscription. The rules of the purpose decide when users start to receive the emails.
