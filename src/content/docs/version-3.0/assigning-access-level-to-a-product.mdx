Every [product](product) requires an associated access level. For subscriptions and lifetime products, the purchase activates that access level: Adapty determines the subscription duration and uses it as the access level’s expiration date, and a lifetime purchase leaves the access level active with no expiration date.

Consumable and non-subscription purchases don’t activate an access level, even though the product still carries one. Those purchases appear in the `nonSubscriptions` array of the user profile, and your app decides what to unlock.

To link an access level to a product:

1. While [configuring a product](create-product), select the access level from the **Access Level ID** list.
