## Before you start

{/* SDK floors: 3.5.0 for paywall tests, 4.0.0 for flow tests; users on older SDKs skip the test.
    At least two placements of one kind (paywall or flow; onboarding excluded), at least two variants,
    at least two pieces of content. Placements can't mix kinds. */}

## How Adapty assigns variants

{/* New users only: never called getPaywall or getFlow. First placement the user meets fixes the variant.
    Cross-device sync: on foregrounding, at most once per 60 seconds. Stickiness: 90 days after the test
    stops, adjustable in General settings. Priority: over regular and onboarding tests; among Crossplacement
    tests, creation order; narrower audiences first. Link the selection-logic diagram from ab-test-types. */}

## Create a Crossplacement A/B test

{/* Same Create A/B test window as a regular test. The Variants table: rows are variants, columns are
    placements; adding a second placement makes the test Crossplacement. Weight per variant, not per
    content item. Variant names are required for Crossplacement tests (blank falls back to Variant A, B…).
    Save as draft or run. Screenshot: variants grid with two flow placements. */}

## Stop a Crossplacement A/B test

{/* Three outcomes chosen per placement: the winning content, another content item not in the test,
    or nothing specific (audience priority decides). Irreversible. Stickiness keeps serving assigned
    users for 90 days. Pending ADP-7253 stand for labels. */}
