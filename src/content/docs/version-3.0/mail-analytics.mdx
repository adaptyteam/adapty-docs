The **Analytics** page shows what your emails did: how many were delivered, opened, and clicked, and how much revenue they brought in. Every number can be broken down — by campaign, by segment, by A/B variant, and more.

The page has a chart on top and a breakdown table below. Click any row to drill into a single entity.

<ZoomImage id="mail-analytics-page.webp" width="700px" alt="Adapty Mail Analytics page with the chart on top and the metrics breakdown table below" />

:::tip
To ask about your numbers in plain language — "which campaign converted best last month?" — connect an AI assistant to the [Adapty Mail MCP server](mail-mcp). It reads the same metrics and breakdowns as this page.
:::

## Pick a time range

The toolbar at the top of the page controls the time window and how it's bucketed:

- **Date range**: Presets (Last 7 / 14 / 30 / 90 days, This month, Last month, Last 12 months, Year to date) or a **Custom range** picker. The default is the last 30 days.
- **Event date** / **Send date**: Choose the day each event counts on. **Event date**, the default, counts every event on the day it happened. **Send date** counts every open, click, and purchase on the day its email was sent, so each day shows the results of that day's emails.
- **Granularity**: **Daily**, **Weekly**, or **Monthly** buckets. Granularity coarsens automatically when the range grows — **Daily** switches to **Weekly** past 92 days, and both switch to **Monthly** past 366 days.
- **Chart style**: **Line**, **Area**, or **Bar**.

If the page reports that the range is too wide, narrow the date range, coarsen the granularity, group by less, or apply filters.

## Group, break down, filter

Three controls below the toolbar shape what the chart and table show:

- **Group by**: Splits the table into one row per campaign, per segment, or whatever else you pick. With **No grouping**, everything collapses into a single **All** row.
- **Breakdown**: Splits each of those rows again, by a second choice. Expand a row to see them. It stays disabled until you set a **Group by**, and you can't pick the same thing twice.
- **Add filter**: Narrows the chart and the table down to the campaigns, segments, or domains you care about.

All three offer the same twelve choices:

| Option | One row per |
| --- | --- |
| **Campaigns** | campaign |
| **Campaign type** | marketing or transactional |
| **Purposes** | [purpose](mail-purposes) the campaign is written for |
| **Email language** | language the email went out in |
| **Segments** | segment the recipient matched |
| **A/B variants** | variation the recipient was routed to |
| **Messages** | individual email in a campaign |
| **Email number** | position of the email in its campaign's sequence: **Email 1**, **Email 2**, and so on |
| **Sender email domain** | sending domain that carried the email |
| **Mailbox email provider** | recipient's mailbox provider — Google, iCloud, and so on |
| **Mailbox email domain** | recipient's email domain |
| **Event outcome** | bounce reason, complaint kind, or link clicked |

Two of those choices behave differently:

- **Messages** appears only in the **Breakdown** dropdown. A subject line means nothing outside its campaign, so group by **Campaigns** first, then split the row of each campaign into its individual emails.
- **Event outcome** gives each row a single kind of event, so most of its columns are empty — a bounce reason has no opens. Rows with nothing to show for the metrics you've selected are hidden. With the **Rate** metric category on, **Event outcome** isn't offered at all, because its rows have nothing to divide by.

## Read the chart

The chart renders the metrics you select over the chosen time range.

- **Metric category**: Toggle between **Email actions** (Sent, Delivered, Unique recipients, Opened, Clicked, Bounced, Complained, Unsubscribed, Unique purchases), **Revenue**, and **[Rate](#plot-a-rate)**. **Unique purchases** is the name of **Converted** in the chart and in the metrics table.
- **Metric pills**: Pick which metrics to plot. In aggregate mode (no grouping), you can plot multiple metrics on the same chart. With grouping on, the chart plots a single metric — one line per group — so groups stay visually distinct.
- **Legend**: When grouping is on, the legend on the right lists every group. You can show or hide the line of each group there.

The visibility checkboxes in the metrics table below also control which rows appear on the chart.

<ZoomImage id="mail-charts.gif" width="700px" alt="Chart updating as the metric, grouping, and chart style change" />

## Plot a rate

To see one metric as a share of another, select the **Rate** metric category. The chart then plots each rate as a percentage per period. Each rate is a chip above the chart. To plot a rate, click its chip. With no grouping, you can plot several rates at once. With grouping on, the chart plots one rate, with one line per group.

Every project starts with three rates:

| Rate | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| **Open rate** | Unique openers / Delivered |
| **Click rate** | Unique clickers / Delivered |
| **Conversion rate** | Unique purchases / Delivered |

The same rates are also columns in the [metrics table](#read-the-metrics-table) and on the [A/B Tests page](mail-ab-testing#read-results). There, a rate divides the totals of the whole time range, not of one period.

Rates read best with **Send date** selected in the [toolbar](#pick-a-time-range). Each day then divides the opens, clicks, and purchases of that day's emails by those same emails. With **Event date**, a day mixes in emails sent earlier, so a rate can drift or go above 100%.

A period where the denominator is zero has no rate. The line breaks there instead of dropping to 0%.

### Change the rates

Rates belong to the project, so a change you save applies to every member of the project.

1. Edit the list:
    - To change a rate, click the pencil icon on its chip, then edit its **Name**, **Numerator**, and **Denominator**.
    - To add a rate, click **Add rate**.
    - To delete a rate, click the pencil icon on its chip, then click **Remove rate**.
2. Review the chart. It shows your changes at once, before you save them.
3. To keep the changes, click **Save for everyone**. To drop them, click **Discard**.

The **Numerator** and **Denominator** can each be one of: Sent, Delivered, Unique recipients, Opened, Unique openers, Clicked, Unique clickers, Bounced, Complained, Unsubscribed, and Unique purchases. Terms that start with **Unique** count people. The other terms count emails. Revenue isn't available in a rate.

**Save for everyone** stays disabled until the list follows these rules:

- **At least one rate**: You can't delete the last rate.
- **A name for every rate**: Up to 64 characters.
- **Two different terms**: A rate can't divide a metric by itself.
- **A unique formula**: Two rates can't use the same numerator and denominator.

## Read the metrics table

Below the chart, the **Message metrics** table lists one row per group. The **All** row at the top adds up every other row in the table.

- **Columns**: Sent, Delivered, Opened, Clicked, and Unique purchases come first. Then the table has one column for each rate of your project, in the order of the rate list. Revenue, Bounced, Complained, and Unsubscribed come last. To see the formula of a rate, hover over the info icon next to its name. To change the rates, see [Change the rates](#change-the-rates).
- **Sort**: Click a column name to sort the table by that column. Click it again to reverse the order.
- **Visibility checkbox**: Toggle whether a row appears on the chart.
- **Expand row**: When a **Breakdown** is set, the chevron on the left of each row expands it into its sub-groups.
- **Open the drilldown**: Click a row's name to open the focused view for that entity.

<ZoomImage id="mail-metrics.webp" width="700px" alt="Message metrics table grouped by campaign with one row expanded into messages, and a column for each project rate" />

The drilldown shows:

- The same chart and metric selector as the main page, scoped to one entity.
- The same metrics table as the main page, with one row for the entity.

If you open the drilldown from a row of a report that has a **Breakdown**, the drilldown splits the entity by that breakdown. The chart shows one metric, with one line for each sub-group, and the table has one row for each sub-group.

The date range and granularity carry over from the main page. Use **Back** in the breadcrumb to return.

## What's tracked

<UsdConversion />

Each row — on the Analytics page, in the drilldown, and in the inline views described below — exposes the same set of raw counts:

- **Sent**: Emails dispatched to SES.
- **Delivered**: SES-confirmed inbox deliveries.
- **Bounced**: SES-reported bounces. Hard and soft bounces both count as one **Bounced**. Each sending domain has a [**Bounces** card](mail-domain-health#bounces) that shows them separately for the last 30 days.
- **Opened**: Opens that Adapty Mail registers when the tracking pixel loads. Apple Mail Privacy Protection pre-fetches images on iOS 15+ and inflates this count — rely on clicks and revenue for stronger signals.
- **Clicked**: Clicks on the CTA button, the hero image, or the logo. Footer links — unsubscribe, privacy, terms — aren't counted, and don't appear anywhere in analytics. A click is recorded at your own [link subdomain](mail-sending-domain#1-add-the-domain), which every link passes through before redirecting onward.
- **Unsubscribed**: Unsubscribes from the footer link or the `List-Unsubscribe` header.
- **Converted**: Unique profiles in the group with an attributed purchase in the time range. With **Event date**, conversions are bucketed by the purchase date — a click in March followed by a buy in April counts in April. With **Send date**, they're bucketed by the day the email was sent. To switch, use the [toolbar](#pick-a-time-range). A profile that purchases more than once still counts once.
- **Revenue**: Sum of attributed revenue (USD) across subscription starts, renewals, and one-time purchases.

## Revenue attribution

Revenue is attributed on **last click**:

1. When a recipient clicks the CTA, the hero image, or the logo, Adapty Mail remembers which email the click came from — for one hour.
2. A purchase in that hour is credited to that email, provided it has no attribution already and happened after the click.
3. A purchase more than an hour after the click, or with no click before it, stays unattributed.
4. A renewal of a product the profile already bought is never credited, however recently they clicked.

Attribution needs nothing extra from your checkout page: the click is recorded at the redirect, and the purchase is matched by profile once it reaches Adapty Mail. The `{email}` and profile-ID placeholders a self-hosted page can carry personalize what the visitor sees; they play no part in attribution. [Set up checkout](mail-checkout) covers both.

## Inline analytics in Flows and A/B Tests

The same metrics also appear inline on the **Flows** and **A/B Tests** pages:

- **Flows page**: Each flow has one row, with **Sent**, the first three rates of your project, and **Revenue** for the content the flow sends now. For a flow that runs an A/B test, the row adds up all variations.
- **A/B Tests page**: Each test has one row, with the numbers of its variations added up. To [compare the variations](mail-ab-testing#read-results), select **View in analytics** in the row menu.

Use the Analytics page when comparing across campaigns or drilling into a single entity, and the inline views when you're already working in a flow or an A/B test. The metric definitions and attribution rules above apply in all three views. The rates differ: the Analytics page and the A/B Tests page show [your project's rates](#plot-a-rate), and the Flows page shows its own fixed rates.

## Limitations

- **All bounces are counted together**: The **Bounced** metric includes both hard and soft bounces. Each sending domain has a [**Bounces** card](mail-domain-health#bounces) that shows them separately for the last 30 days.
- **Eventual consistency, not real-time**: Counts are aggregated from event tables. Fresh events usually appear within minutes, but there's no streaming guarantee.
- **Range size is capped**: A wide date range with fine granularity, or a grouping with many rows, can exceed what the chart will render. The page then shows *"Selected range is too wide. Try a coarser granularity or shorter range."*
- **No language data for older events**: **Email language** shows every event before October 6, 2026 as **English**, including those from translated emails.
