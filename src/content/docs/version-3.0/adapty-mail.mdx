Adapty Mail turns your Adapty user data into AI-generated email sequences that convert trial users into paid subscribers. It uses the profile data already in your Adapty project to build, send, and attribute campaigns — no separate email platform required.

## Why Adapty Mail?

Sending targeted email campaigns requires copy, design, sending infrastructure, and revenue attribution. Each of these is a separate problem to solve.

Adapty Mail handles all of it. Your brand profile is built from your store URL and any other sources you add, and a complete email sequence is generated from it in under 2 minutes — sent from your own domain with personalized checkout links and purchase attribution.

## How it works

1. **Collect emails**: Your app passes user emails and `customer_user_id` values to Adapty via SDK. Adapty Mail uses this data to identify recipients and attribute revenue to the specific email that drove each purchase. You can also send this data from your server with the [Adapty Mail API](mail-send-data-via-api). Adapty Mail matches people by email address. Two sources reporting the same address land on one profile; two different addresses create separate profiles, even when `customer_user_id` is identical.
2. **Build a web paywall**: The checkout page every email links to.
3. **Generate a sequence**: AI uses your brand profile to produce 1–15 emails — copy, design, hero images, and personalized checkout links matched to your app's category and brand voice.
4. **Launch a flow**: Create a flow: pick its purpose (such as **Never purchased**, **Renewal cancelled**, **Expired**, **Billing issue**, or **Refunded**) and a segment, then select your campaign. Emails start sending automatically, and revenue from email-driven purchases is attributed back to the specific email that drove the conversion.

## Requirements

Every Adapty Mail project needs:

- **Sending domain**: A domain you control, with access to its DNS settings. You [add it as a root domain](mail-sending-domain), such as `yourapp.com`.
- **A checkout to send people to**: Every email links to your [checkout page](mail-checkout).
- **User identity**: A stable user ID and an email address for each user. These are what Adapty Mail delivers to and attributes revenue with — missing either one leaves that user out of sends and out of campaign analytics.

Then, depending on how you set it up:

- **Stripe**, if you want the bundled paywall builder to generate your checkout. To use another payment provider, host the checkout yourself and point Adapty Mail at its URL.
- **An [entry point](mail-entry-points)** that brings you user data — the Adapty SDK if your app runs it, the Adapty Mail API if your backend is the source of truth, or FunnelFox if you sell through web funnels.

## Get started

Follow the [Get started with Adapty Mail](mail-get-started) guide to complete setup and launch your first campaign. Once setup is complete, [test your flow](mail-testing) before you launch to real users.

To set up and run Adapty Mail from Claude, ChatGPT, or a coding agent, connect the [Adapty Mail MCP server](mail-mcp). In a coding agent, the [Adapty Mail skill](mail-skill) adds a confirmation before anything that emails real people.
