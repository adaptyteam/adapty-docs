Adapty reads your products and validates purchases as the service account. Until you invite that account into Google Play Console and grant it permissions, it has no access to your app's data.

## Before you start

- **Previous step**: [Create a service account](create-service-account)
- **Why**: Adapty needs a service account to communicate with Google's APIs on your behalf. You need to know that account's email address to grant it the necessary permissions.

## Grant the permissions

1. Open [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console/) and go to **Users and permissions**.

   <ZoomImage id="gp-users-and-permissions.webp" width="700px" alt="Users and permissions page in Google Play Console with the Invite new users button" />

2. Click **Invite new users**.

3. In **Email address**, paste the service account's email address.

4. Open the **Account permissions** tab. Permissions granted here apply to every app in your developer account, while the **App permissions** tab covers only the apps you select individually.

   <ZoomImage id="gp-account-permissions.webp" width="700px" alt="Account permissions tab on the Invite user page, showing the permission checkboxes" />

5. Select these four permissions:

   - View app information and download bulk reports (read-only)
   - View financial data, orders, and cancellation survey responses
   - Manage orders and subscriptions
   - Manage store presence

6. Click **Invite user**, then confirm with **Send invite**. The service account appears in the user list.
