The adapty-attribution skill lets an AI coding tool answer questions about your ad campaigns from your [Adapty Attribution](adapty-user-acquisition) data. Ask in plain language — "Which channel paid back best last month?", "Should we kill this campaign?" — and it runs the [`adapty attribution` commands](developer-cli-attribution), reads the results, and answers with the numbers behind the verdict.

A skill is a set of instructions your AI tool loads on demand. This one contains the parts of the Attribution reports that are easy to misread: which revenue basis a ROAS is on, which values can't be computed yet, and where Apple Search Ads spend comes from.

:::note
The skill only reads. It changes no campaign, tracking link, or integration, and the budget moves it suggests are yours to make.
:::

## What it answers

The skill works from playbooks — a set of commands, a way to read them, and the shape of the answer — for the questions teams ask most:

- **Payback and budget**: Ranks channels and campaigns by ROAS and says where extra budget should go, and in what steps.
- **Young campaigns**: Judges a new campaign at a day its cohorts have reached, instead of on a D30 that has only a week of revenue in it.
- **Trial quality**: Ranks campaigns by cost per paying trial rather than cost per install, so campaigns whose trials don't convert show up.
- **Weekly digest**: Compares the last week with the ones before it and flags what moved.
- **Countries and stores**: Shows where to expand and where to cap spend.
- **Winners inside a campaign**: Ranks ad sets and ads within one campaign.
- **Low-quality traffic**: Flags campaigns with more refunds and chargebacks than the app's average.
- **Predicted payback**: Reads predicted ROAS at longer horizons and turns it into a break-even CPI.
- **Numbers that disagree**: Explains why Meta, TikTok, or Google report a different ROAS than Adapty.
- **Installs dropped**: Reads the pattern of a drop to tell an app-side cause from a single network's.

Setup questions — connecting Meta, TikTok, or Google Ads, tracking links, the web pixel, Stripe, or Paddle — go to the docs pages that own them.

## Apple Search Ads

Attribution doesn't collect Apple Search Ads spend, so its ROAS is empty there. The skill gets Apple Search Ads spend and ROAS from [Ads Manager](developer-cli-ads-manager) and reports them as their own line, in your campaign group's currency, instead of merging them into the Attribution numbers. If you give it a spend amount yourself, it answers with a threshold — "Apple Search Ads beats Google if its spend was under $X" — rather than a ROAS built from your estimate.

## Install the skill

The skill is part of the [`adapty-skills` plugin](https://github.com/adaptyteam/adapty-skills).

<AdaptySkillsInstall />

The skill needs the [Adapty CLI](developer-cli-quickstart), logged in, and Attribution access for your company. Then ask your question in plain language — see the example prompts below.

## Example prompts

Name the app, the period, and the decision you're making. The skill works out the commands.

```
Our app is app_finance. Which paid channels paid back best in August, and where
should next month's extra $20k of budget go? Judge on revenue after the store's
cut.
```

```
Our TikTok "Fall Promo" campaign started on Sep 15. Leadership's rule is to kill
anything under 80% D30 ROAS. How is it doing against "Spring Sleep", and should
we kill it?
```

```
Meta Ads Manager says our "Broad US" campaign had a 2.4 purchase ROAS in August.
Adapty shows something much lower. Which number is right, and what do I tell my
boss?
```
