Transfer your app to a different owner when your company is acquired, you're selling your app, or reorganizing business entities. After the transfer, the new owner [checks that purchases unlock access](#verify-transfer-completed-successfully).

## Transfer app ownership

:::note
Do not delete or recreate products during the transfer process. Do not change product IDs until after verifying the transfer completed successfully.
:::

Follow the steps for each account that changes:

- The Apple developer account: [App Store (iOS) transfer](#app-store-ios-transfer)
- The Google Play developer account: [Google Play (Android) transfer](#google-play-android-transfer)
- Only the Adapty account: [Adapty-only transfer](#adapty-only-transfer)

### App Store (iOS) transfer

If the app moves to another Apple developer account, the new owner replaces three credentials in Adapty with their own:

- The In-App Purchase key
- The App Store Connect API key (a team key)
- The app-specific shared secret

The app keeps its Bundle ID and its notification URL in Adapty.

The transfer has two phases: [before Apple completes it](#before-apple-completes-the-transfer) and [after Apple completes it](#after-apple-completes-the-transfer).

#### Before Apple completes the transfer

1. **New owner:** If you don't have an Adapty account, create one at [app.adapty.io](https://app.adapty.io).
2. **Old owner:** Initiate the app transfer in App Store Connect following Apple's [transfer guide](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/transfer-an-app/overview-of-app-transfer).
3. **Old owner:** Email [support@adapty.io](mailto:support@adapty.io) to transfer the app in Adapty. Include the app name and the email address of the Adapty account from step 1. Then the new owner can enter their own credentials for the app in Adapty.
4. **New owner:** You need an In-App Purchase key and a team key with **Admin** access from your App Store Connect team. If you have the file of an existing active key, use that key. Otherwise, generate the [In-App Purchase key](generate-in-app-purchase-key#generate-the-key-in-app-store-connect) or the [team key](app-store-connect-api-key#generate-a-team-key-in-app-store-connect) in App Store Connect.
5. **New owner:** Accept the transfer in App Store Connect. Apple completes it within two business days, then notifies both Account Holders.

#### After Apple completes the transfer

:::warning
Save the new In-App Purchase key in Adapty as soon as Apple completes the transfer. The old owner's key stops working for the app after the transfer. While Adapty has no working key, new purchases unlock no access, and the app receives no error. Users who buy during that time get access when they [restore purchases](troubleshoot-app-store-integration#a-purchase-succeeds-but-the-customer-loses-access).
:::

1. **New owner:** Go to [**App settings** > **iOS SDK**](https://app.adapty.io/settings/ios-sdk) in Adapty. Enter the new In-App Purchase key in the **In-app purchase API (StoreKit 2)** and **App Store promotional offers** sections, and click **Save**.
2. **New owner:** Connect the team key, so that Adapty keeps your products in sync with App Store Connect. If the **App Store Connect API key** section already has a key, click **Edit**. Otherwise, click **Connect API Key**. Then enter the team key.
3. **New owner:** In App Store Connect, open the **App Information** page of the app. Check that [**Production Server URL** and **Sandbox Server URL**](enable-app-store-server-notifications#enter-the-notification-url-in-app-store-connect) there match the **URL for App Store server notifications** in Adapty.
4. **New owner:** Once [the new keys work](app-store-connection-configuration#check-each-credential), [regenerate the app-specific shared secret](app-store-connection-configuration#enter-the-app-specific-shared-secret) so that the old owner's copy stops working. Paste the new secret into Adapty right away, and click **Save**.

### Google Play (Android) transfer

If the app moves to another Google Play developer account, the new owner replaces every Google Play credential in Adapty with their own.

1. **New owner:** If you don't have an Adapty account, create one at [app.adapty.io](https://app.adapty.io).
2. **Both owners:** Ensure both Google Play Developer accounts are fully registered.
3. **Old owner:** Submit a transfer request via Google Play Console or Google Play Developer Support. Google may request additional documents such as DUNS numbers, contracts, or proof of sale.
4. **New owner:** Review and approve the transfer request.
5. **Google:** Google support team processes the transfer, typically within a few business days, but may take longer depending on account verification, subscription complexity, and payment setup.
6. **Old owner:** After Google completes the transfer, email [support@adapty.io](mailto:support@adapty.io) to transfer the app in Adapty. Provide the app name and new owner's email address.
7. **New owner:** After receiving the app in Adapty, complete the [Google Play integration guide](initial-android) to generate and configure all credentials under your account.

The transfer includes users, subscriptions, statistics, ratings, and store listing. Billing continuity is maintained for existing subscribers, but payouts switch to the new owner's merchant account only after the transfer completes. Payout reports and orders before transfer remain in the original account. Follow Google's [transfer guide](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247) for detailed requirements.

### Adapty-only transfer

If the app only moves to another Adapty account, it keeps every credential and notification URL. The stores and the app's code need no changes.

1. **New owner:** If you don't have an Adapty account, create one at [app.adapty.io](https://app.adapty.io).
2. **Old owner:** Email [support@adapty.io](mailto:support@adapty.io) to transfer the app in Adapty. Provide the app name and new owner's email address.

## Verify transfer completed successfully

After the transfer, the new owner checks the app:

1. **Check Dashboard access**: New owner should see the app in their Adapty Dashboard.
2. **Test SDK connection**: Run your app and verify Adapty SDK initializes without errors.
3. **Test a purchase**: Make a [test purchase](validate-test-purchases), and check that it unlocks access.
4. **Check credentials**: For the App Store, [check that each credential works](app-store-connection-configuration#check-each-credential). For Google Play, check that the new service account connects successfully in Adapty.
5. **Check renewals**: In the days after the transfer, renewals from existing subscribers should appear in the [**Event feed**](event-feed).
